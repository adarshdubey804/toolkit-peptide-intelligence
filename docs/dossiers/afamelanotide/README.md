# Afamelanotide (SCENESSE; Melanotan I)

> Workbook entry: **111/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

FDA-approved on 8 October 2019 to increase pain-free light exposure in adults with a history of phototoxic reactions from erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP). Approval is limited to EPP, not cosmetic tanning.

## Identity and chemistry

Synthetic linear tridecapeptide analogue of α-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH), with N-terminal acetylation and C-terminal amidation. Commonly reported sequence: Ac-Ser-Tyr-Ser-Nle-Glu-His-D-Phe-Arg-Trp-Gly-Lys-Pro-Val-NH₂. Verify notation and stereochemistry against product documentation before canonical registration.

## Mechanism of action

Agonism at melanocortin-1 receptor (MC1R) on melanocytes increases eumelanin production and photoprotective pigmentation. In EPP, this can improve tolerance of visible-light exposure and reduce phototoxic symptoms; it does not correct the underlying heme-biosynthesis defect.

## Pharmacodynamics

Clinical evidence supports increased pain-free light exposure in adults with EPP. It is symptomatic photoprotection, not a disease-modifying correction of ferrochelatase deficiency.

## Pharmacokinetics

Administered as a subcutaneous biodegradable implant. Obtain quantitative PK and release data from approved labeling/regulatory documents rather than inferring them from implant interval.

## Clinical evidence

Evidence applies to adults with EPP and prior phototoxic reactions. Do not extrapolate to ordinary tanning or unrelated photosensitivity disorders.

## Safety and tolerability

Label precautions include skin examination/monitoring and implant-related adverse effects; nausea, headache and implant-site reactions have been reported. Consult current label for complete safety profile.

## Regulatory status

FDA-approved as SCENESSE on 8 October 2019 for adult EPP patients with prior phototoxic reactions. No cosmetic tanning indication.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Controlled-release biodegradable subcutaneous implant. Composition, release characteristics and storage should be sourced from approved product documentation.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Relevant analyses: MC1R signalling, melanogenesis networks, porphyrin/heme biosynthesis (including FECH), visible-light phototoxicity and patient-reported outcomes.
