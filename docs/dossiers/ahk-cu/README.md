# AHK-Cu

> Workbook entry: **123/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Copper(II) complex of the tripeptide Ala-His-Lys, promoted in cosmetic contexts for hair and skin. It is not FDA-approved as a drug for hair growth, wound healing or dermatologic disease.

## Identity and chemistry

Peptide ligand AHK (Ala-His-Lys) coordinated with copper(II). Metal coordination/speciation depends on pH, stoichiometry and formulation; record the peptide ligand and copper complex separately. Verify sequence stereochemistry and complex composition analytically.

## Mechanism of action

Proposed cosmetic mechanisms include effects on dermal papilla cells, extracellular-matrix remodelling and hair-follicle biology. Evidence is limited and often extrapolated from GHK-Cu or other copper peptides; a validated AHK-Cu-specific molecular target and human mechanism are not established.

## Pharmacodynamics

No robust clinical pharmacodynamic dataset or validated concentration-response relationship for AHK-Cu hair-growth claims. In-vitro and marketing claims should not be treated as clinical endpoints.

## Pharmacokinetics

No approved drug label or validated human PK profile. Topical penetration and local copper exposure depend on vehicle, skin condition and complex stability; systemic exposure is not characterized.

## Clinical evidence

No high-quality independently replicated clinical evidence establishes AHK-Cu efficacy for hair regrowth or treatment of alopecia. Distinguish ingredient-level cosmetic data from controlled trials of a defined formulation.

## Safety and tolerability

Human safety data for defined AHK-Cu products are limited. Copper content, free copper, impurities, irritation, sensitization and stability should be characterized. Cosmetic topical use does not establish safety of injectable or systemic use.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved as a drug. Cosmetic ingredient marketing is distinct from FDA drug approval; claims to treat disease or alter body structure/function may change regulatory classification.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Appears in topical cosmetic formulations. No FDA-approved medicinal formulation or standardized injectable product specification. Report AHK concentration, copper content/speciation, free copper, pH and stability.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential research: copper coordination chemistry, dermal papilla transcriptomics, hair follicle cycling, extracellular matrix and oxidative stress. Keep AHK-Cu distinct from GHK-Cu and other copper complexes.
