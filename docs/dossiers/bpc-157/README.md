# BPC-157

> Workbook entry: **113/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Synthetic 15-residue peptide marketed for tendon, gut and wound recovery. It is not FDA-approved; marketing claims exceed the available human evidence.

## Identity and chemistry

Commonly reported sequence: GEPPPGKPADDAGLV (15 amino acids), described as a fragment derived from a gastric protein. Exact provenance and structure should be checked against primary chemistry sources; avoid treating commercial materials as authenticated native human peptides.

## Mechanism of action

Preclinical studies report effects on angiogenesis, nitric-oxide signalling, growth-factor pathways and tissue repair models. No validated molecular target or established human mechanism has been demonstrated; describe proposed pathways as preclinical hypotheses.

## Pharmacodynamics

Most pharmacodynamic findings are from animal or cell models involving tendon, ligament, gastrointestinal and wound-healing endpoints. Human exposure and concentration-response relationships are not adequately characterized.

## Pharmacokinetics

No approved product label or robust human PK profile. Stability, bioavailability, metabolism and route-dependent exposure remain insufficiently characterized.

## Clinical evidence

Human evidence is sparse and does not establish efficacy or safety for musculoskeletal injury, inflammatory bowel disease or general tissue repair. Preclinical outcomes cannot be extrapolated to clinical benefit.

## Safety and tolerability

FDA identifies potential immunogenicity risks for certain routes and complexities involving peptide-related impurities and API characterization; the agency reports limited safety information for proposed routes. Quality and sterility of unregulated products are additional concerns.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved. FDA has identified BPC-157 among bulk substances raising significant safety concerns during compounding review. Compounding-list review or policy discussion does not equal drug approval.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Unapproved materials are marketed in multiple forms, but no standardized FDA-approved formulation, validated manufacturing specification or authorized product quality standard exists.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential research: angiogenesis, endothelial signalling, nitric-oxide pathways, extracellular matrix remodelling and tissue-repair transcriptomics. All proposed mechanisms require rigorous independent validation.

## Evidence gaps and curation actions

- Verify exact molecular form, termini, sequence and salt against primary analytical/regulatory records.
- Add primary clinical citations and extract trial design, sample sizes, endpoints and adverse events.
- Distinguish evidence for a fragment from evidence for its parent protein or related peptides.
- Confirm current jurisdiction-specific regulatory status from primary regulator registers.
