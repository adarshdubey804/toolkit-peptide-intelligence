# Bremelanotide (VYLEESI; PT-141)

> Workbook entry: **110/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

FDA-approved in 2019 for acquired, generalized hypoactive sexual desire disorder (HSDD) in premenopausal women, subject to defined exclusions and limitations. It is not approved as a general sexual-performance enhancer, for men or postmenopausal women.

## Identity and chemistry

Synthetic cyclic melanocortin peptide; PT-141 is a development name. Exact sequence/chemical representation should be imported from a curated primary chemical source with stereochemistry and cyclization explicitly encoded; do not represent it as native α-MSH.

## Mechanism of action

Nonselective melanocortin receptor agonist with activity at MC1R, MC3R, MC4R and MC5R; labeling reports greatest potency at MC1R and MC4R. The mechanism by which it improves HSDD is unknown. Central MC4R pathways are biologically relevant but do not establish a definitive therapeutic circuit mechanism.

## Pharmacodynamics

Clinical evaluation used patient-reported desire and distress outcomes in the indicated population. Benefits should not be equated with enhanced sexual performance.

## Pharmacokinetics

Subcutaneous administration. Extract Cmax, Tmax, half-life, metabolism and exposure data from current prescribing information before quantitative modelling.

## Clinical evidence

The label-defined population excludes HSDD attributable to coexisting medical/psychiatric conditions, relationship problems or medication/substance effects. Do not generalize beyond studied populations.

## Safety and tolerability

Label risks include transient blood-pressure increases, contraindication in uncontrolled hypertension or known cardiovascular disease, focal hyperpigmentation and nausea. Review the current label for full precautions.

## Regulatory status

FDA-approved as VYLEESI in 2019 for acquired, generalized HSDD in premenopausal women, with explicit limitations of use.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Commercial subcutaneous injection in a prefilled delivery system. Device, formulation and stability information should be taken from approved labeling.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential analyses: melanocortin receptor selectivity, MC4R CNS expression, receptor-ligand modelling and pharmacovigilance. Receptor activation is not proof of the clinical mechanism.
