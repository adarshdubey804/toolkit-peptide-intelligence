## 4. Mechanism of action

Amylin is a pancreatic beta-cell hormone co-secreted with insulin. Amylin acts through receptor complexes formed by the calcitonin receptor (CTR) and one of the receptor activity-modifying proteins (RAMP1, RAMP2 or RAMP3).

Cagrilintide is a long-acting amylin analogue intended to activate amylin receptor signalling. Mechanistic effects relevant to energy balance include:
1. signalling in central pathways involved in satiation and appetite;
2. reduced food intake and increased meal-related satiety;
3. modulation of gastric emptying;
4. downstream effects on body-weight regulation.

Do not describe cagrilintide as a GLP-1 receptor agonist. In CagriSema, semaglutide supplies GLP-1R agonism while cagrilintide supplies amylin analogue activity. Do not attribute the combination's total effect to cagrilintide alone.

- Cagrilintide monotherapy remains investigational as of 3 November 2026.
- Novo Nordisk lists cagrilintide (NN9833) in Phase 3 for obesity.
- The RENEW programme evaluates cagrilintide monotherapy in people with obesity/overweight, with and without type 2 diabetes.

## 10. Clinical development

### Phase 2 monotherapy — NCT03856047
A multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo- and active-controlled dose-finding trial assessed once-weekly subcutaneous cagrilintide (0.3, 0.6, 1.2, 2.4 and 4.5 mg) in adults with overweight/obesity without diabetes, with liraglutide 3.0 mg and placebo comparator groups.

The primary publication reported dose-dependent body-weight reduction over 26 weeks. Extract the full efficacy estimates, confidence intervals, estimands, dose titration and adverse-event tables directly from the paper before loading exact numerical claims.

### Phase 3 monotherapy — RENEW
- **RENEW 1:** Phase 3 study in adults with obesity/overweight without type 2 diabetes; once-weekly cagrilintide versus placebo, with lifestyle intervention.
- **RENEW 2:** Phase 3 study in adults with obesity/overweight and type 2 diabetes.
- The sponsor's public programme materials report initiation of RENEW 1 and RENEW 2 in November 2025.
- Trial registry identifiers, protocol versions, recruitment status and primary completion dates must be verified from ClinicalTrials.gov and CTIS before final database import.
- A Phase 3 presentation-comparability study, protocol NN9833-8550, is registered in CTIS as 2025-524249-29-00; it compares two injectable cagrilintide presentations with placebo.

### Cagrilintide plus semaglutide — CagriSema (separate combination evidence)
CagriSema is relevant for interaction/combination context but must not be conflated with monotherapy:
- **REDEFINE 1, NCT05567796:** Phase 3a, 68 weeks, adults with overweight/obesity without diabetes. Cagrilintide 2.4 mg plus semaglutide 2.4 mg was compared with semaglutide alone, cagrilintide alone and placebo.
- **REDEFINE 2, NCT05394519:** Phase 3a, 68 weeks, adults with overweight/obesity and type 2 diabetes.
- The REDEFINE 1 paper reports estimated mean body-weight change of −20.4% with combination versus −3.0% with placebo at week 68 under the treatment-policy estimand; this is combination efficacy, not cagrilintide monotherapy efficacy.
- The REDEFINE 2 paper reports −13.7% versus −3.4% at week 68 for combination versus placebo; again, this is combination efficacy.
