# Cerebrolysin

> Workbook entry: **119/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Standardized porcine-brain-derived peptide and amino-acid mixture used as a medicinal product in some countries for neurological conditions. It is not FDA-approved or centrally authorized by the EMA. It is a complex mixture, not one peptide molecule.

## Identity and chemistry

Enzymatic hydrolysate of porcine brain proteins; commonly described as approximately 80% low-molecular-weight peptides and 20% free amino acids. Reported peptidomic analyses identify hundreds of peptide species. A single sequence, molecular formula or molecular weight is therefore not appropriate.

## Mechanism of action

Proposed neurotrophic and neuroprotective actions include modulation of neuronal survival, synaptic plasticity and responses to ischemic injury. The active molecular constituents and definitive mechanism remain unresolved; claims of NGF/BDNF-like activity should be qualified.

## Pharmacodynamics

Preclinical work reports neuronal survival and reduced injury in cell/animal models. Clinical outcomes vary by indication and trial; no single validated pharmacodynamic marker captures the mixture's activity.

## Pharmacokinetics

Conventional single-molecule PK parameters are not directly applicable to this heterogeneous mixture. Peptide composition, proteolytic stability, biodistribution and exposure-response are incompletely characterized.

## Clinical evidence

Cochrane reviews have assessed cerebrolysin in acute ischemic stroke and vascular dementia. Evidence is uncertain/low certainty for several patient-important outcomes; review conclusions and safety signals should be considered indication-specific. Do not present widespread regional use as proof of efficacy.

## Safety and tolerability

Adverse-event and mortality findings differ across indication and trial set. Use primary trial and systematic-review data for each indication; avoid broad claims of safety. Porcine origin and parenteral administration require product-specific quality and allergy/contraindication review.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved and not centrally EMA-authorized. Marketed/registered in a number of countries, but country-specific registration and indications should be verified against local official registers.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Commercial preparation is a sterile solution for injection/infusion in jurisdictions where registered. Manufacturing relies on controlled porcine-brain sourcing and enzymatic hydrolysis, followed by purification and standardization; exact process is manufacturer-controlled.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Peptidomics, proteomics, batch-comparability, peptide-family annotation, bioactivity-guided fractionation and systems-level neurotrophic pathway analysis are more appropriate than single-sequence modelling.

## Evidence gaps and curation actions

- Verify molecular identity, sequence, stereochemistry, termini and product form against primary analytical/regulatory records.
- Extract primary clinical trial designs, sample sizes, endpoints and adverse-event results; do not rely on marketing summaries.
- For mixtures and metal complexes, preserve compositional/speciation complexity rather than forcing a single peptide structure.
- Confirm jurisdiction-specific regulatory status from current official registers.
- Keep this dossier **in_progress** until claim-level review is complete.
