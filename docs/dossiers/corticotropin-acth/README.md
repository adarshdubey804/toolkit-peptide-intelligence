# Dossier 081 — Corticotropin (ACTH; Acthar Gel / Purified Cortrophin Gel)

**Workbook entry:** 081 | **Category:** Neurology, Pain & CNS | **Workbook first approval field:** 1952 (Acthar) | **Status:** FDA-approved products  
**Curation:** `in_progress`

## Identity and product distinction
Corticotropin is adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH), a 39-amino-acid peptide produced by pituitary corticotrophs from pro-opiomelanocortin (POMC). The N-terminal 1–24 region contains the principal melanocortin receptor activating sequence; the C-terminal residues contribute to the full hormone's properties. Commercial products differ:
- **Repository corticotropin injection (Acthar Gel):** a complex porcine pituitary-derived preparation with product-specific composition and gel formulation.
- **Purified corticotropin (Cortrophin Gel):** a separately manufactured purified ACTH preparation.
Do not assume these products are compositionally or clinically interchangeable solely because both are labelled corticotropin.

## Sequence and chemistry
Human ACTH(1–39) sequence: `SYSMEHFRWGKPVGKKRRPVKVYPNGAEDESAEAFPLEF`. The N-terminal active fragment ACTH(1–24) is `SYSMEHFRWGKPVGKKRRPVKVYPN`. Full sequence is useful for human endogenous ACTH biology, but it must not be assigned as the exact molecular composition of a porcine-derived complex product without product-specific characterization. Acthar Gel is not a single sequence-defined synthetic peptide.

## Mechanism
ACTH activates melanocortin 2 receptor (MC2R) in the adrenal cortex. MC2R couples to Gs, activating adenylyl cyclase, cAMP and PKA, promoting steroidogenesis and cortisol production. ACTH can also influence melanocortin receptors in extra-adrenal tissues, but the contribution of these pathways to every labelled therapeutic indication is not fully resolved. Corticosteroid-mediated anti-inflammatory effects are an important component of clinical pharmacology. Do not claim the gel's clinical effects are explained exclusively by adrenal cortisol stimulation.

## Clinical evidence and indications
Acthar Gel has multiple historical labelled indications, including selected acute exacerbations of multiple sclerosis and infantile spasms, as well as specified inflammatory and autoimmune conditions. Cortrophin Gel has its own label and indications. These products have different manufacturing histories and should have separate regulatory/product records. Historical approval dates should not be collapsed into a single universal ACTH approval date.

For infantile spasms, ACTH has evidence from clinical trials and guidelines, but regimens, formulations and comparator evidence differ. For MS exacerbations, ACTH is an established historical treatment option in selected settings; it is not a disease-modifying therapy. For other labelled conditions, evaluate indication-specific evidence and modern comparator context separately.

## Pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics
ACTH stimulates adrenal steroidogenesis; serum cortisol and other adrenal steroids are pharmacodynamic readouts. Depot/gel formulations produce product-specific absorption profiles. A single half-life should not be assigned across repository corticotropin and purified corticotropin products. Exact PK is difficult to infer from endogenous ACTH physiology and must be product-label-specific.

## Safety
Important risks reflect corticosteroid excess and product-specific hypersensitivity. Potential adverse effects include infection susceptibility, adrenal suppression, hypertension, fluid retention, hypokalaemia, hyperglycaemia, gastrointestinal complications, mood changes and effects on growth in children. Contraindications and warnings vary by product label. For infantile spasms, monitor treatment response and serious infection/adrenal complications under specialist care. Do not extrapolate a single product's warning list to all ACTH products.

## Product quality
Acthar Gel is a complex animal-derived peptide preparation and must not be modelled as a pure 39-residue ACTH molecule. Critical attributes include source material controls, identity/potency, peptide composition, biological activity, impurities, adventitious-agent controls, sterility, endotoxin, gel properties and stability. Purified Cortrophin Gel has separate product-specific quality attributes and regulatory records.

## Regulatory status
Workbook records Acthar's first US approval field as 1952. Modern label indications and the status of Purified Cortrophin Gel must be checked product-by-product in Drugs@FDA and current DailyMed labels. The 1952 historical entry is not evidence that every contemporary ACTH product or indication has identical approval history.
