# Dasiglucagon

> Workbook entry: **016/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Long-acting glucagon analogue marketed as ZEGALOGUE for treatment of severe hypoglycaemia in adults and children with diabetes. Product-specific approval and labeling must be distinguished from native glucagon.

## Identity and chemistry

Modified 29-amino-acid glucagon analogue with seven amino-acid substitutions designed to improve peptide stability in aqueous formulation. Use the current FDA label's sequence/structural depiction for exact residue-by-residue encoding; do not infer substitutions from generic secondary descriptions.

## Mechanism of action

Agonist at the glucagon receptor (GCGR), activating cAMP signalling and hepatic glycogenolysis/gluconeogenesis to increase blood glucose. Its stabilizing substitutions improve solution stability compared with native glucagon.

## Pharmacodynamics

Rapid pharmacodynamic increase in plasma glucose following subcutaneous administration; exact response magnitude and time course should be taken from current product label and trial datasets.

## Pharmacokinetics

Subcutaneous ready-to-use formulation. Extract label PK parameters and study conditions before quantitative modelling; analogue stability does not imply identical PK to native glucagon.

## Clinical evidence

FDA approval was based on controlled studies in adults and pediatric patients with diabetes experiencing insulin-induced hypoglycaemia. Analyze endpoints and age groups from ZEGALOGUE label and regulatory review.

## Safety and tolerability

Common adverse reactions include nausea, vomiting, headache and injection-site reactions. Hypersensitivity and product-specific warnings should be extracted from the current label.

## Regulatory status

FDA-approved as ZEGALOGUE in 2021 for severe hypoglycaemia in adults and children with diabetes. Check current label for exact age eligibility and product-specific use.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Ready-to-use aqueous subcutaneous autoinjector and prefilled syringe; label lists dasiglucagon hydrochloride, tromethamine, sodium chloride and water for injection.
