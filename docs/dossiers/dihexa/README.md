# Dihexa

> Workbook entry: **118/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Experimental angiotensin-IV-derived peptidomimetic investigated in preclinical cognition and neuroregeneration models. It is not an FDA-approved medicine and has no established clinical efficacy.

## Identity and chemistry

Often described as N-hexanoic-Tyr-Ile-(6-aminohexanoic acid)-? in secondary sources; exact connectivity and complete chemical identity must be resolved from the original patent and primary analytical records before sequence/SMILES registration. Do not encode an inferred peptide sequence.

## Mechanism of action

Reported to bind hepatocyte growth factor (HGF) and potentiate HGF/c-Met signalling in experimental systems, with effects on synaptogenesis and neurite outgrowth. This is preclinical pharmacology, not an established human target-engagement mechanism.

## Pharmacodynamics

Preclinical studies report changes in synaptic density, neurite growth and memory-related behavioural outcomes in animal models. No validated human pharmacodynamic biomarkers or dose-response relationship exists.

## Pharmacokinetics

No approved label or reliable human PK profile. Bioavailability and exposure claims from animal studies should not be extrapolated to people or unregulated formulations.

## Clinical evidence

No adequate controlled human efficacy programme establishes benefit for cognitive impairment, dementia or neurodegeneration. Do not infer human benefit from rodent memory studies.

## Safety and tolerability

Human safety is not established. Long-term effects of potentiating HGF/c-Met signalling, including effects on proliferative pathways, require careful investigation; this is a theoretical/preclinical concern, not a quantified clinical risk.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved; no verified major-regulator marketing authorization identified.

## Formulation and manufacturing

No standardized authorized medicinal formulation or validated GMP product specification. Research materials require identity, purity, stability and impurity characterization.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential research: HGF/c-Met signalling, synaptogenesis, neurite outgrowth, synaptic proteomics and neurodegeneration models. Confirm molecular identity before cheminformatics calculations.
