# Elamipretide (FORZINITY; SS-31)

> Workbook entry: **109/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

FDA accelerated approval on 19 September 2025 to improve muscle strength in adults and pediatric patients with Barth syndrome weighing at least 30 kg. FORZINITY is the approved product; unapproved online SS-31 material is not equivalent to an authorized medicine.

## Identity and chemistry

Mitochondria-targeted aromatic-cationic tetrapeptide, also called SS-31 or MTP-131. Commonly reported sequence is D-Arg-dimethylTyr-Lys-Phe-NH₂; verify stereochemistry and dimethyltyrosine notation against a primary chemical record before computational registration.

## Mechanism of action

Binds cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane and is proposed to stabilize cristae/respiratory-chain organization, support electron transport and improve mitochondrial bioenergetics. FDA labeling describes it as a mitochondrial cardiolipin binder; broader mechanisms should be labelled proposed unless directly demonstrated.

## Pharmacodynamics

The FDA evidence package included a small randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study and a long-term open-label extension. Accelerated approval relied on improvement in knee-extensor muscle strength, an intermediate endpoint; confirmatory evidence is required.

## Pharmacokinetics

Subcutaneous administration. Use the current label for quantitative PK and renal-impairment details; do not estimate parameters from peptide class.

## Clinical evidence

The pivotal Barth syndrome study enrolled only 12 patients, reflecting disease rarity. Interpret endpoint and extension findings with sample-size and open-label limitations.

## Safety and tolerability

Injection-site reactions are common. Label warnings include benzyl alcohol toxicity and precautions for renal impairment. Consult current label for full safety information.

## Regulatory status

FDA accelerated approval 19 September 2025 for Barth syndrome in patients weighing ≥30 kg. Investigational SS-31 in other conditions is not thereby approved.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Defined subcutaneous injectable formulation. Label provides strength, route and excipient-related warnings; commercial CMC details may be proprietary.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Relevant work: cardiolipin-binding structural analysis, tafazzin/Barth syndrome biology, mitochondrial respiratory-chain networks, lipidomics, mitochondrial proteomics and oxidative-phosphorylation signatures.
