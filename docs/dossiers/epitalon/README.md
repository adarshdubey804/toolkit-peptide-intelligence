# Epitalon (Epithalon)

> Workbook entry: **124/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Synthetic tetrapeptide marketed for sleep and anti-aging, based on research into pineal peptide preparations. It is not FDA-approved and no robust clinical evidence establishes anti-aging or lifespan benefit.

## Identity and chemistry

Commonly reported tetrapeptide sequence: Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly (AEDG). Distinguish synthetic Epitalon/Epithalon from the historical pineal peptide preparation epithalamin, which is a mixture and not chemically identical.

## Mechanism of action

Proposed effects on pineal function, melatonin regulation and telomere/telomerase biology appear in preclinical and small exploratory reports. A validated molecular target and reproducible human mechanism have not been established.

## Pharmacodynamics

Claims include changes in melatonin secretion, circadian rhythms and telomerase activity, but evidence is heterogeneous and not sufficient to establish clinically meaningful anti-aging pharmacodynamics.

## Pharmacokinetics

No approved product label or reliable human PK profile. Absorption, metabolism, tissue distribution and CNS exposure remain inadequately characterized.

## Clinical evidence

Published human reports are limited, often small or methodologically difficult to evaluate, and do not establish lifespan extension, prevention of age-related disease or durable sleep benefit. Do not present animal longevity findings as human outcomes.

## Safety and tolerability

Long-term safety, carcinogenicity and repeat-dose tolerability are not adequately characterized. Claims involving telomerase biology require particular caution and do not establish either benefit or harm in humans.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved; no verified major-regulator marketing authorization identified. Online products marketed for longevity are not authorized medicines.

## Formulation and manufacturing

No standardized authorized medicinal formulation or validated GMP product specification. Research materials require identity, purity, aggregation, endotoxin and sterility assessment.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential analyses: circadian biology, pineal signalling, melatonin pathways, telomere/telomerase regulation and aging-related transcriptomics. Treat all longevity claims as unproven.

## Evidence gaps and curation actions

- Verify exact molecular identity, stereochemistry, termini, variant and any cell-penetrating segment against primary analytical records.
- Add primary clinical citations and extract study design, sample sizes, endpoints and adverse-event results.
- Separate endogenous biology, animal experiments, biomarker associations and administered-product clinical evidence.
- Confirm jurisdiction-specific regulatory status from current official registers.
