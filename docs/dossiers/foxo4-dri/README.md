# FOXO4-DRI

> Workbook entry: **127/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Experimental cell-penetrating peptide designed to disrupt FOXO4-p53 interaction in senescent cells. It is a preclinical senolytic research compound, not an approved medicine.

## Identity and chemistry

FOXO4-DRI is a synthetic D-retro-inverso peptide based on a FOXO4 interaction motif, commonly reported as a cell-penetrating construct. Published descriptions include a D-amino-acid retro-inverso FOXO4-derived segment fused to a penetratin-like sequence; exact full sequence and linker/terminal chemistry must be copied from the primary paper or patent, not reconstructed from the name.

## Mechanism of action

Designed to interfere with the interaction between FOXO4 and p53 in senescent cells, promoting p53 nuclear exclusion and apoptosis in experimental models. The reported senolytic mechanism is preclinical and does not establish selectivity or efficacy in humans.

## Pharmacodynamics

Mouse studies reported reductions in senescence-associated markers and functional improvements in selected accelerated-aging and naturally aged models. These findings require independent replication and are not human pharmacodynamic evidence.

## Pharmacokinetics

No approved label or validated human PK profile. Cell penetration and animal exposure do not establish human distribution, metabolism or tissue selectivity.

## Clinical evidence

No adequate human clinical trial evidence establishes safety or efficacy of FOXO4-DRI for aging, fibrosis, cancer or any other condition. Animal senolysis is not a clinical anti-aging treatment.

## Safety and tolerability

Human safety is unknown. Systemic interference with senescence and apoptosis pathways could have complex effects on tissue repair, tumor suppression and normal cell homeostasis; these are biological concerns requiring study, not quantified clinical outcomes.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved; no verified major-regulator marketing authorization identified.

## Formulation and manufacturing

No standardized authorized medicinal formulation or validated GMP specification. Research material requires exact sequence, stereochemistry, cell-penetrating segment and impurity characterization.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential research: FOXO4-p53 protein interaction, senescence-associated secretory phenotype, apoptosis, cell-state transcriptomics and tissue-specific senescence biomarkers.
