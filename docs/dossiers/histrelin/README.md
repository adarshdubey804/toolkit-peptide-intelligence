# Dossier 028 — Histrelin (histrelin acetate)

**Curation status:** in progress — current US SUPPRELIN LA label and FDA GSRS identity record reviewed; historical VANTAS indication/status, complete clinical evidence, global regulatory status and product CMC need further review.  
**Asset class:** synthetic modified nonapeptide GnRH analogue.  
**Pharmacology:** GnRH receptor agonist; continuous delivery suppresses pituitary gonadotropins after an initial stimulatory phase.  
**Current US reference product:** SUPPRELIN LA, 50 mg histrelin acetate subcutaneous implant, designed for approximately 65 micrograms/day over 12 months.  
**Historical US product:** VANTAS, 50 mg histrelin acetate implant for advanced prostate cancer; do not assume current marketing availability.

## 1. Identity and classification

Histrelin is a synthetic, modified nonapeptide analogue of gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH/LHRH). It is supplied clinically as histrelin acetate. Its distinctive substitutions include an N-benzylated D-histidine at position 6 and a C-terminal proline N-ethylamide. The current SUPPRELIN LA FDA label identifies the drug as a GnRH agonist and gives its complete chemical description. [1,2]

## 2. Mechanism of action

Histrelin is an agonist at pituitary GnRH receptors. Its endocrine action is biphasic:

1. **Initial stimulation:** GnRH receptor activation transiently increases LH and FSH, which can briefly increase gonadal steroid concentrations.
2. **Continuous exposure:** sustained histrelin delivery causes reversible downregulation of pituitary GnRH receptors and desensitisation of gonadotroph cells.
3. **Downstream suppression:** LH and FSH fall, reducing ovarian or testicular steroidogenesis. In children with central precocious puberty (CPP), this suppresses premature pubertal progression and slows the accelerated linear growth velocity associated with untreated CPP.

The current SUPPRELIN LA label reports that LH response can fall to prepubertal levels within about one month of treatment. The label explicitly describes the initial stimulatory phase followed by reversible pituitary GnRH receptor downregulation. [1]

## 3. Molecular structure and structure–activity features

The peptide is a nine-residue GnRH analogue. Its sequence is represented as:

`pGlu-His-Trp-Ser-Tyr-N-benzyl-D-His-Leu-Arg-Pro-NHEt`

Structural modifications:
- **N-terminus:** pyroglutamyl (pGlu).
- **Position 6:** N-benzyl-D-histidine, replacing the native glycine residue and introducing both D stereochemistry and an N-benzyl substituent.
- **C-terminus:** proline N-ethylamide.
- **Salt:** the SUPPRELIN LA label describes histrelin acetate as a diacetate representation.

Do not confuse histrelin with leuprolide or goserelin: all are GnRH agonists, but their sequences and residue modifications differ. The current label's full chemical name is the controlling source for this dossier's sequence representation. [1,3]

## 4. Receptor pharmacology and pharmacodynamics

The target is the pituitary GnRH receptor, a class A GPCR. GnRH receptor activation engages Gq/11-associated phospholipase-C signalling, intracellular calcium mobilisation and downstream pathways regulating gonadotropin synthesis and secretion. Continuous agonist exposure produces pituitary desensitisation/downregulation, decreasing LH and FSH and consequently sex-steroid production.

For SUPPRELIN LA, the label reports suppression of LH response to GnRH to prepubertal levels within approximately one month, with corresponding decreases in oestrogen or testosterone. The clinical pharmacodynamic objective in CPP is cessation or regression of progressive secondary sexual development and reduction of pubertal growth velocity. [1]

Quantitative binding affinity, receptor EC50, intrinsic efficacy, species comparison and signalling bias are not asserted here; a suitable primary comparative dataset has not yet been extracted.

## 5. Pharmacokinetics and delivery system

### SUPPRELIN LA

SUPPRELIN LA is a **non-biodegradable, diffusion-controlled hydrogel reservoir implant**. Each implant contains 50 mg histrelin acetate and is designed to release approximately 65 micrograms/day for 12 months. It is inserted subcutaneously in the inner upper arm and is removed/replaced at the end of the treatment interval according to the label. [1]

The current label reports clinical PK data in 47 children with CPP. Median serum histrelin concentrations remained above the assay limit of quantification during the treatment period; the median of maximum serum concentrations over the study period was 0.43 ng/mL. This is a depot-exposure profile and must not be interpreted as the intrinsic elimination half-life of free histrelin. [1]

### VANTAS (historical product)

VANTAS was a 50 mg histrelin acetate subcutaneous implant developed for palliative treatment of advanced prostate cancer. Its product history is distinct from SUPPRELIN LA, even though both use the same active substance and a year-long implant concept. Current US marketing/distribution status and the latest product-specific label should be verified before representing VANTAS as currently available. [2,4]

## 6. Clinical uses and evidence

### Central precocious puberty

SUPPRELIN LA is FDA-indicated for treatment of children with central precocious puberty. Diagnosis and monitoring are based on clinical progression, growth, bone age and biochemical evidence of gonadotropin/sex-steroid suppression. The label requires implant insertion and removal by a qualified healthcare professional. [1]

The label's clinical pharmacology section documents sustained serum exposure and suppression of LH response. A complete evidence summary should additionally extract pivotal trial design, number of children, treatment duration, biochemical suppression proportions, growth velocity, bone-age progression, adult-height outcomes and implant-related complications from the clinical review and original publications. Those detailed values are not reproduced here.

### Advanced prostate cancer — historical VANTAS indication

VANTAS was approved as a histrelin acetate implant for palliative treatment of advanced prostate cancer. Its historical use relies on the same continuous GnRH agonist principle: initial hormonal stimulation followed by sustained suppression of LH and testosterone. This historical indication must not be attributed to SUPPRELIN LA's current CPP label. [4]

## 7. Regulatory status

**United States — SUPPRELIN LA:** FDA-approved for treatment of children with central precocious puberty. The current DailyMed label lists initial US approval in 1991 and describes the 50 mg, 12-month implant. [1]

**United States — VANTAS:** a separate histrelin acetate implant was approved for palliative treatment of advanced prostate cancer. FDA historical product records identify NDA 021732 and an initial approval year of 2004. This dossier does not assert that VANTAS remains commercially marketed. [4,5]

**Other jurisdictions:** not exhaustively verified. National approval, current marketing status and indication must be checked separately; US approval does not imply global authorisation.

## 8. Safety and tolerability

For SUPPRELIN LA, safety includes both expected GnRH agonist endocrine effects and implant/procedure-specific risks. The current label includes warnings for:
- initial gonadotropin and sex-steroid surge, with possible transient worsening of pubertal signs;
- implant-site reactions, bruising, pain, swelling, infection or scarring;
- implant breakage, migration, extrusion, difficult removal or retained fragments;
- inadequate suppression or return of pubertal signs if delivery is interrupted;
- hypersensitivity reactions;
- severe cutaneous adverse reactions, added to the label in September 2025;
- potential changes in bone mineral density and growth parameters during sex-steroid suppression.

The current label's safety data and monitoring recommendations should be extracted in full before populating frequency estimates. This summary is not a substitute for the product label or clinical care. [1]

For historical VANTAS, prostate-cancer-specific flare and androgen-deprivation risks must be evaluated from its own label and clinical context; they should not be mixed into the SUPPRELIN LA paediatric safety table.

## 9. Formulation, implant and CMC

SUPPRELIN LA is a sterile, non-biodegradable hydrogel polymer reservoir approximately 3.5 cm long and 3 mm in diameter, containing a 50 mg histrelin acetate drug core. The reservoir provides diffusion-controlled release. The drug core also contains stearic acid. The label describes the hydrogel reservoir as containing methacrylate-based polymer components and identifies a hydrated presentation in a glass vial containing sterile saline. [1,3]

CMC attributes to curate:
- peptide sequence, D-histidine stereochemistry and N-benzyl substitution;
- N-terminal pyroglutamate and C-terminal N-ethylamide;
- acetate stoichiometry and peptide content;
- drug-core excipient identity and uniformity;
- hydrogel polymer composition, dimensions and mechanical integrity;
- diffusion/release rate and 12-month delivery consistency;
- residual monomers/initiators and extractables/leachables;
- sterility, endotoxin and container-closure integrity;
- implant insertion tool performance and retrieval integrity;
- shelf life and hydrated storage stability.

The hydrogel implant is not a biodegradable PLGA microsphere; do not copy formulation details from other GnRH depot products.
