# Humanin

> Workbook entry: **126/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Mitochondrial-derived 24-amino-acid peptide first identified in neuroprotective research. It is a research molecule, not an FDA-approved therapeutic; clinical efficacy and safety of administered humanin are unestablished.

## Identity and chemistry

Humanin is commonly reported as a 24-amino-acid peptide: MAPRGFSCLLLLTSEIDLPVKRRA. Sequence variants and species-specific forms exist; record the exact variant and source organism. Do not conflate native humanin with analogues such as HNG/S14G-humanin.

## Mechanism of action

Preclinical studies suggest cytoprotective signalling involving formyl-peptide receptor family members (including FPR2/ALX in some models) and intracellular interactions with pro-apoptotic proteins such as Bax. Receptor dependence varies by model and is not a single fully resolved mechanism.

## Pharmacodynamics

Experimental work reports effects on apoptosis, oxidative stress, inflammation and metabolic signalling in cellular and animal systems. No validated human pharmacodynamic endpoint for therapeutic humanin administration exists.

## Pharmacokinetics

No approved product label or validated human PK profile. Endogenous peptide measurement and pharmacokinetics of an administered synthetic peptide are distinct questions.

## Clinical evidence

Human studies have largely evaluated circulating humanin as a biomarker or association with health/metabolic phenotypes. They do not establish therapeutic efficacy of administered humanin for neurodegeneration, cardiovascular disease or aging.

## Safety and tolerability

Safety of exogenous humanin, analogues and chronic administration is not established. Immunogenicity, stability, tissue distribution and potential effects across multiple survival pathways require investigation.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved; no verified major-regulator marketing authorization identified.

## Formulation and manufacturing

No standardized authorized medicinal formulation. Research preparations require sequence/variant authentication, purity, aggregation and stability testing.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential research: mitochondrial-derived peptide signalling, apoptosis networks, FPR2/ALX, Bax/Bcl-2 interactions, metabolic phenotyping and multi-omics biomarker analysis.
