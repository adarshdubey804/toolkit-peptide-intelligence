# Dossier 103 — Icotrokinra (Icotyde)

## Identity and molecular chemistry
Icotrokinra is a first-in-class orally administered peptide antagonist of the interleukin-23 receptor (IL-23R). The approved product is ICOTYDE, supplied as 200 mg film-coated tablets; the active ingredient is icotrokinra, present as the hydrochloride salt in the drug substance. It is an orally bioavailable targeted peptide rather than a conventional small-molecule cytokine inhibitor. The full sequence, stereochemistry and molecular formula should be transcribed from the FDA chemistry review/official structural record; do not infer a linear sequence from the mechanism or secondary vendor descriptions.

## Mechanism of action
Icotrokinra selectively binds IL-23R and competitively antagonizes IL-23 binding. The FDA label reports a dissociation constant (Kd) of 7 pM. Blocking IL-23/IL-23R signalling reduces release of downstream pro-inflammatory cytokines involved in psoriasis immunopathology. It acts at the receptor rather than neutralizing circulating IL-23 ligand. This distinction is central to its target annotation.

## Pharmacology and administration context
The approved tablet is administered orally once daily under label-specific fasting conditions. Oral absorption is formulation- and food-dependent; preserve the label's administration constraints in the structured product record rather than treating it as a generic oral peptide. ## Clinical evidence and indication
ICOTYDE is indicated for moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults and paediatric patients aged 12 years and older who weigh at least 40 kg and are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy. FDA approval in March 2026 was supported by four randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trials involving approximately 2,500 participants, with efficacy assessments at week 16. Do not generalize the indication to psoriatic arthritis, inflammatory bowel disease or other IL-23-mediated disorders.

## Safety
The label warns that immunomodulatory treatment may increase infection risk and includes evaluation for tuberculosis and immunization considerations. Capture exact contraindications, warnings, adverse-event frequencies, laboratory monitoring and drug-interaction information from the current full label. Do not assume that the safety profile is identical to injectable IL-23 monoclonal antibodies; the molecule, route and exposure are distinct.
