# Melanotan II (MT-II)

> Workbook entry: **112/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Unapproved synthetic melanocortin research peptide. It is not an FDA-approved tanning medicine or approved treatment for sexual dysfunction. Do not confuse it with afamelanotide (Melanotan I/SCENESSE), which has a narrow EPP indication.

## Identity and chemistry

Synthetic cyclic heptapeptide melanocortin analogue, commonly described as Ac-Nle-c[Asp-His-D-Phe-Arg-Trp-Lys]-NH₂. Encode lactam connectivity and stereochemistry explicitly; validate against primary chemistry literature before registering a structure.

## Mechanism of action

Nonselective melanocortin receptor agonist. Experimental pharmacology includes MC1R and central melanocortin receptors such as MC3R/MC4R. Pigmentation and other reported effects are pharmacological observations, not evidence of safe or approved therapeutic use.

## Pharmacodynamics

Human evidence is limited and heterogeneous, including small experimental studies. There is no FDA-approved efficacy package supporting online marketing claims for tanning or libido.

## Pharmacokinetics

No approved human product, standardized formulation or validated clinical PK label. Online products may vary in identity, purity, concentration and sterility; no reliable product PK should be inferred.

## Clinical evidence

Separate MT-II from afamelanotide (Melanotan I), bremelanotide (PT-141) and other melanocortin ligands. Shared pharmacophore does not imply interchangeable efficacy, safety or regulatory status.

## Safety and tolerability

Unapproved products create risks from uncertain identity, purity, concentration and sterility. Literature and regulator reports describe concerns including nausea, flushing, spontaneous erections/priapism and pigmentary changes; strength of evidence varies. This dossier does not provide use or dosing guidance.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved. Not an authorized cosmetic tanning drug or approved sexual-dysfunction treatment. Check local medicines authority records for jurisdiction-specific controls.

## Formulation and manufacturing

No standardized authorized formulation or GMP product specification. Research material cannot be assumed equivalent to online vials, sprays or compounded preparations.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential analyses: melanocortin receptor binding/selectivity, ligand conformation, MC1R/MC3R/MC4R signalling and pharmacovigilance. Flag uncertain product provenance in any dataset.
