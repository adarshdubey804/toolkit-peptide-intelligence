# Navepegritide (YUVIWEL)

> Workbook entry: **108/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

FDA accelerated approval on 27 February 2026 to increase linear growth in pediatric patients aged 2 years and older with achondroplasia and open epiphyses. This is not a general growth-promoting indication.

## Identity and chemistry

A long-acting C-type natriuretic peptide (CNP) analogue engineered for sustained systemic exposure over a weekly interval. Exact sequence, linker/conjugate architecture and stereochemistry should be transcribed from primary chemistry/regulatory records; not inferred here.

## Mechanism of action

Activates natriuretic peptide receptor-B (NPR-B/NPR2), a particulate guanylyl cyclase, increasing cGMP and downstream PKG signalling. In growth-plate chondrocytes, CNP signalling counteracts FGFR3-mediated suppression of endochondral ossification. This pathway rationale is established CNP biology; product-specific causal detail should be distinguished from class inference.

## Pharmacodynamics

Accelerated approval was based on improvement in annualized growth velocity. Confirmatory evidence of clinical benefit remains required. Weekly dosing is designed to sustain CNP exposure across the interval.

## Pharmacokinetics

Once-weekly subcutaneous product. Extract numerical exposure, half-life and disposition parameters directly from the current label before quantitative modelling.

## Clinical evidence

Evidence and eligibility apply to children ≥2 years with achondroplasia and open epiphyses. Do not extrapolate to closed growth plates, other skeletal dysplasias or non-achondroplasia short stature.

## Safety and tolerability

Use the current YUVIWEL label for adverse reactions, warnings, contraindications and monitoring. This dossier intentionally does not provide dosing instructions.

## Regulatory status

FDA-approved as YUVIWEL under accelerated approval on 27 February 2026; NDA 219164. Jurisdiction- and indication-specific.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Sterile injectable powder for reconstitution for subcutaneous administration. Detailed CMC, excipient and stability data require label/CMC review.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential analyses: NPR2/cGMP/PKG signalling, FGFR3 interaction, growth-plate transcriptomics and exposure-response modelling. Separate measured product effects from pathway hypotheses.
