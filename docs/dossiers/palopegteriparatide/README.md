# Palopegteriparatide (YORVIPATH)

> Workbook entry: **044/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Long-acting prodrug of PTH(1-34), FDA-approved in 2024 for treatment of hypoparathyroidism in adults. It releases active PTH(1-34) under physiological conditions; product-specific PEG/linker architecture is essential to identity.

## Identity and chemistry

Palopegteriparatide is a prodrug conjugate of PTH(1-34) linked to a carrier through a self-cleaving linker, designed to provide sustained release of active PTH(1-34). Do not represent it as unmodified teriparatide or assign the molecular mass of PTH(1-34) to the full conjugate. Use the approved label/CMC sources for exact conjugate chemistry and PEG architecture.

## Mechanism of action

At physiological conditions, the conjugate releases PTH(1-34), which activates PTH1R on osteoblasts, osteocytes and renal tubular cells. PTH1R signalling regulates calcium and phosphate homeostasis through effects on bone turnover, renal calcium reabsorption, phosphate excretion and active vitamin D synthesis.

## Pharmacodynamics

Continuous systemic PTH(1-34) exposure is intended to provide more physiologic replacement than intermittent short-acting PTH exposure. Label describes dose-related increases in PTH(1-34) and serum calcium in healthy volunteers.

## Pharmacokinetics

Once-daily subcutaneous pen formulation. Quantitative PK must be taken from the current YORVIPATH label, distinguishing released PTH(1-34) from intact conjugate where measured.

## Clinical evidence

FDA approval on 9 August 2024 was based on a randomized, placebo-controlled 26-week phase 3 trial of 82 adults with hypoparathyroidism (Study NCT04701203). Not studied for acute postsurgical hypoparathyroidism.

## Safety and tolerability

Important risks include serious hypercalcemia and hypocalcemia, hypersensitivity reactions, orthostatic hypotension and potential osteosarcoma risk in susceptible patients; concomitant digoxin requires attention to hypercalcemia-related toxicity. Use current label.

## Regulatory status

FDA-approved as YORVIPATH on 9 August 2024 for adults with hypoparathyroidism. Product-specific indication does not extend automatically to acute postsurgical hypoparathyroidism or pediatric populations.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Sterile clear solution in single-patient-use prefilled pen. Current label describes palopegteriparatide concentration expressed as teriparatide-equivalent PTH(1-34), plus mannitol, metacresol, succinic acid, sodium hydroxide and water for injection.
