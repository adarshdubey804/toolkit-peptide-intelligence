# Dossier 101 — Pegcetacoplan (Empaveli; Syfovre)

**Workbook entry:** 101 | **Category:** Immunology, inflammation & transplant | **First US approval:** 2021 (Empaveli); 2023 (Syfovre) | **Status:** FDA-approved products  
**Curation:** `in_progress`

## Identity and product distinction
Pegcetacoplan is a pegylated cyclic peptide inhibitor of complement component C3 and its activation fragment C3b. It consists of a cyclic peptide moiety conjugated to polyethylene glycol (PEG) chains. The conjugate is a large, heterogeneous biologic-like molecule; it is not accurately represented by a single simple peptide sequence or a single exact molecular mass without defining PEG distribution and conjugation state.

Two FDA-approved products have different routes and indications:
- **Empaveli:** subcutaneous pegcetacoplan for adults with paroxysmal nocturnal haemoglobinuria (PNH).
- **Syfovre:** intravitreal pegcetacoplan for geographic atrophy secondary to age-related macular degeneration.
Maintain distinct product/route records; do not transfer safety or efficacy claims between them.

## Mechanism
Pegcetacoplan binds C3 and C3b, inhibiting central complement activation. In PNH, C3 inhibition reduces complement-mediated extravascular haemolysis and can improve haemoglobin/haemolysis measures; it does not directly correct the PIGA-mutant haematopoietic clone. In geographic atrophy, local complement inhibition is intended to slow lesion growth; it does not restore lost retinal tissue or establish improved visual acuity as a guaranteed outcome.

## Pharmacology and PK
Empaveli is administered subcutaneously; Syfovre is administered by intravitreal injection. Systemic exposure, distribution and safety are route-dependent. PEGylation prolongs exposure relative to an unconjugated small peptide. Capture route-specific PK from each product label; do not use systemic PNH PK as a surrogate for ocular exposure.

## Clinical evidence
**PNH / Empaveli:** The PEGASUS trial compared pegcetacoplan with eculizumab in adults with PNH and persistent anaemia despite eculizumab; the primary endpoint was change in haemoglobin at week 16. PRINCE evaluated complement-inhibitor-naïve adults. Outcomes include haemoglobin, transfusion avoidance and haemolysis measures.

**Geographic atrophy / Syfovre:** OAKS and DERBY evaluated intravitreal pegcetacoplan versus sham and showed reduced growth of geographic atrophy lesions over 24 months. The pivotal evidence supports slowing lesion enlargement, not restoration of vision. Postmarketing retinal vasculitis and vascular occlusion safety signals require explicit attention.

## Safety
Both products carry serious infection risk related to complement inhibition, including risk from encapsulated bacteria. Vaccination and infection-prevention measures must follow each product's current label; urgent evaluation is needed for suspected serious infection. Empaveli has risks including haemolysis during interruption and injection-site reactions. Syfovre has ocular risks including endophthalmitis, retinal detachment, intraocular inflammation, increased intraocular pressure and retinal vasculitis/vascular occlusion. Route-specific warnings must not be conflated.

## Formulation and quality
Pegcetacoplan is a PEG-peptide conjugate. Critical attributes include peptide identity and stereochemistry, PEG molecular-weight distribution, conjugation site/occupancy, free peptide/free PEG, aggregates, potency for C3 inhibition, impurities, sterility, endotoxin and container/device integrity. Empaveli and Syfovre have distinct sterile formulations and presentations.

## Regulatory status
Empaveli was FDA approved in 2021 for adult PNH. Syfovre was approved in 2023 for geographic atrophy secondary to AMD. Separate labels, applications, routes and benefit-risk profiles must be maintained.
