# Pinealon (EDR)

> Workbook entry: **120/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Short synthetic tripeptide in the Khavinson bioregulator research family, investigated mainly in preclinical neuroprotection models. It is not an approved medicine.

## Identity and chemistry

Commonly reported sequence: Glu-Asp-Arg (EDR). Confirm stereochemistry, termini, salt form and analytical reference before assigning canonical identifiers.

## Mechanism of action

No validated receptor or molecular target has been established. Proposed gene-expression and neuroprotective effects derive largely from experimental cell and animal work and should not be presented as a proven mechanism.

## Pharmacodynamics

Preclinical studies report effects on neuronal stress responses and cellular viability under selected experimental conditions. Human pharmacodynamic evidence is insufficient.

## Pharmacokinetics

No approved product label or reliable human PK profile. Stability, absorption, metabolism and CNS exposure remain unresolved.

## Clinical evidence

No robust independently replicated randomized human trials establish efficacy for neuroprotection, cognition, sleep or age-related disease.

## Safety and tolerability

Human safety and repeat-dose tolerability are not adequately characterized. Unapproved research products have additional identity, purity and sterility uncertainty.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved; no verified major-regulator marketing authorization identified.

## Formulation and manufacturing

No standardized authorized medicinal formulation or approved manufacturing specification.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential work: peptide stability, neuroinflammation, oxidative-stress response, transcriptomics and blood-brain barrier exposure. Clearly label hypotheses and preclinical-only evidence.
