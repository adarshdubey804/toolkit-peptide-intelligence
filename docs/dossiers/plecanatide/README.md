# Dossier 075 — Plecanatide (Trulance)

**Workbook entry:** 075  
**Category:** Gastrointestinal  
**Workbook indication:** IBS-C; chronic idiopathic constipation  
**FDA approval:** 2017  
**Curation status:** `in_progress`

## 1. Identity
Plecanatide is an orally administered 16-amino-acid peptide and GC-C agonist. It is a structural analogue of human uroguanylin. US brand: Trulance. It acts locally on the intestinal epithelium and has minimal systemic exposure.

## 2. Sequence and chemistry
Label-defined sequence: `NDECE LCVNVACTGCL` (without spacing: `NDECELCVNVACTGCL`).  
The 16-residue chain contains disulfide bonds Cys4–Cys12 and Cys7–Cys15. Formula: C65H104N18O26S4. Molecular weight: 1682 Da. The FDA label's structure graphic gives the mature sequence as H-Asn-Asp-Glu-Cys-Glu-Leu-Cys-Val-Asn-Val-Ala-Cys-Thr-Gly-Cys-Leu-OH. Sequence orientation must follow that labelled structure; the chemical name in some label renderings is awkwardly formatted, so the structure graphic is the preferred machine-readable transcription source. Product is a 3 mg oral tablet.

## 3. Mechanism of action
Plecanatide and its active metabolite bind GC-C on the luminal surface of intestinal epithelial cells. GC-C activation raises intracellular and extracellular cGMP. Intracellular cGMP activates CFTR, increasing chloride and bicarbonate secretion; sodium and water follow, increasing intestinal fluid and accelerating transit. Extracellular cGMP may reduce visceral nociceptor activity in animal models, but its clinical contribution is not fully established.

## 4. Pharmacodynamics
The primary effect is intestinal fluid secretion and improved transit. Plecanatide's pH-sensitive activity is proposed to reflect the physiology of uroguanylin, but avoid claiming clinical superiority or a proven pH-selective therapeutic window without comparative human evidence.

## 5. Pharmacokinetics
Systemic concentrations are below the limit of quantification after oral administration at therapeutic doses in most subjects. The peptide is locally degraded within the GI tract. No conventional systemic half-life should be asserted where measurable exposure is absent. The active metabolite remains capable of GC-C activation.

## 6. Clinical evidence
US-approved for adults with CIC and IBS-C. Registration trials were randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled studies using indication-specific composite endpoints: CIC responder status based on sustained increases in complete spontaneous bowel movements (CSBMs), and IBS-C responder status incorporating abdominal pain and CSBM improvement. Use current label tables for exact study-specific rates and definitions; do not combine CIC and IBS-C results into a single efficacy percentage.

## 7. Safety
Diarrhoea is the principal adverse reaction and may be severe. Plecanatide is contraindicated in children younger than 6 years because of serious dehydration risk and in patients with known or suspected mechanical gastrointestinal obstruction. Avoid use in patients aged 6 to less than 18 years; safety and efficacy have not been established in this age group. Other reported reactions include abdominal distension, flatulence, dizziness and upper respiratory tract infection; use the label's exact event tables for incidence.

## 8. Formulation and quality
Trulance is supplied as a 3 mg tablet for oral use. Excipients include microcrystalline cellulose and magnesium stearate. Quality controls should cover peptide identity, purity, potency, correct disulfide pairing, related substances, tablet content uniformity, dissolution, moisture, and stability.

## 9. Regulatory status
Initial US approval: January 2017. Current US label includes adult CIC and IBS-C indications. Verify latest label revision and exact age/contraindication language before publishing clinical decision support.
