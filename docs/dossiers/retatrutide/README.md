## 2. Molecular sequence and structural identity

The unmodified one-letter backbone representation is:

`YAQGTFTSDYSILLDKKAQAAFIEYLLEGGPSSGAPPPS`

This is **not** a complete chemical structure. The molecule contains:
- Aib (2-aminoisobutyric acid / 2-methylalanine) at positions 2 and 20;
- α-methyl-L-leucine at position 13;
- Lys17 side-chain N6 acylation through a γ-glutamyl and AEEA spacer to a C20 fatty diacid;
- C-terminal serinamide.

The USAN systematic name and GSRS substance record are the preferred primary identity references. Preserve the non-natural residue stereochemistry, side-chain conjugation, linker connectivity and C-terminal amide when generating a canonical structure. ## 4. Mechanism of action

Retatrutide is a single molecule designed to activate three class B peptide hormone receptors:

1. **GIP receptor (GIPR):** activation of the incretin receptor contributes to glucose-dependent insulin secretion and metabolic regulation.
2. **GLP-1 receptor (GLP-1R):** activation contributes to glucose-dependent insulin secretion, suppression of inappropriate glucagon secretion, delayed gastric emptying and appetite/satiety effects.
3. **Glucagon receptor (GCGR):** activation engages hepatic glucagon signalling and may increase energy expenditure and alter lipid metabolism; it can also increase hepatic glucose output, making the integrated balance with incretin receptor activity important.

The net pharmacology is not equivalent to administering three separate hormones. It depends on receptor-specific potency, efficacy, tissue distribution, exposure and the balance of agonism across all three receptors.

The discovery programme describes a GIP-based peptide scaffold engineered for balanced multi-receptor activity and prolonged exposure through lipidation. Quantitative potency/selectivity claims must be tied to the original assay system and species; do not compare EC50 values from different assay formats as if directly interchangeable.
