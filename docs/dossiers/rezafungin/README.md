# Dossier 095 — Rezafungin (Rezzayo)

## Identity and chemistry
Rezafungin is a next-generation semisynthetic echinocandin antifungal, structurally related to anidulafungin and engineered for prolonged exposure and chemical stability. It is a cyclic hexapeptide-like lipopeptide with a modified side chain and non-proteinogenic residues. It is not a linear peptide. Capture the complete stereochemical macrocycle, lipid substituent and salt form from the official structure record.

## Mechanism
Rezafungin inhibits β-(1,3)-D-glucan synthase, impairing fungal cell-wall synthesis. It is active against susceptible *Candida* species and has activity against *Aspergillus* in vitro. As with other echinocandins, FKS hotspot mutations can confer resistance. The extended half-life supports once-weekly administration; this pharmacokinetic feature does not imply a different molecular target.

## Pharmacology and PK
Rezafungin is administered intravenously once weekly for the approved indication. It has a prolonged terminal half-life (approximately 133 hours in label pharmacokinetic data), high protein binding and limited renal elimination of unchanged drug. It undergoes slow chemical degradation and metabolism. Review current label for hepatic impairment, drug interactions and exact PK values. Weekly dosing is specific to the approved product and indication; do not extrapolate to other echinocandins.

## Clinical evidence and indication
Rezzayo is indicated in adults with limited or no alternative options for treatment of candidemia and invasive candidiasis. FDA approval in 2023 was based on the phase 3 ReSTORE study, which demonstrated noninferiority to caspofungin on a day-14 global cure endpoint in the studied population. The label restricts use to patients with limited or no alternative options; do not present it as unrestricted first-line replacement for all echinocandins. Evidence for other fungal diseases is not established by this approval.

## Safety
Common adverse reactions include hypokalaemia, fever, diarrhea, nausea, vomiting and infusion-related reactions. Hypersensitivity, hepatic laboratory abnormalities and infusion-related events are important considerations. The long half-life means adverse exposure cannot be rapidly reversed by simply withholding the next dose.

## Formulation and quality
Rezzayo is supplied as sterile lyophilized powder for IV infusion. Critical quality attributes include macrocycle identity, side-chain structure, stereochemical integrity, potency, related substances, degradation, residual solvents, water content, reconstitution, sterility, endotoxin, particulate matter and container closure.
