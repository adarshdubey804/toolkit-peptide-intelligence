The FDA lists synthetic porcine secretin (SecreFlo) as approved for secretin stimulation testing, including pancreatic secretion and ERCP-related uses. FDA orphan-product records show an approval date of 1 November 2002 for the ERCP indication; separate FDA records show the gastrinoma and pancreatic exocrine-function approvals in April 2002. Human synthetic secretin (ChiRhoStim) has an initial US approval in 2004 and a current DailyMed label with NDA 021256.

## 2. Molecular identity and sequence

**Human secretin (ChiRhoStim):**
- 27-residue peptide hormone, C-terminally amidated.
- Sequence as printed in the FDA label:  
  `HSDGTFTSELSRLREGARLQRLLQGLV-NH2`
- Molecular formula: C130H220N44O39.
- Molecular weight: 3,039.44 Da.
- CAS Registry Number: 108153-74-8.
- FDA UNII: A0426J905J.
- Supplied as the acetate salt in the commercial product.

**Porcine secretin (SecreFlo):**
- 27-residue peptide, C-terminally amidated.
- The FDA pharmacology review describes the synthetic porcine product as sequence-identical to naturally occurring porcine secretin.
- Do not copy the human sequence into the porcine record: species-specific residue differences must be preserved. The exact porcine sequence and validated sequence accession are a curation task before completion.

The hormone family is structurally related to other secretin/glucagon-family peptides, including VIP, GIP, glucagon and GHRH. Family membership does not imply interchangeable receptor pharmacology.

## 3. Physiological role

Endogenous secretin is released from duodenal enteroendocrine S cells in response to acidic gastric contents entering the proximal small intestine. It helps coordinate neutralization of acid by stimulating bicarbonate-rich pancreatic and biliary fluid secretion. It also participates in regulation of gastric and intestinal physiology.

## 4. Mechanism of action

**Primary mechanism — secretin receptor signalling in pancreatic ductal cells:**

1. Secretin binds the secretin receptor (SCTR), a class B G-protein-coupled receptor, on pancreatic ductal epithelial cells.
2. Receptor activation principally couples to Gs and stimulates adenylyl cyclase, increasing intracellular cAMP.
3. cAMP-dependent signalling promotes CFTR channel activity and coordinated ion/fluid transport.
4. The result is secretion of a high-volume, bicarbonate-rich pancreatic fluid into the ductal system and duodenum.

The FDA label specifically describes secretin receptor binding on pancreatic duct cells and CFTR-channel opening as the basis for bicarbonate-rich fluid secretion. It also notes a possible vagal–vagal contribution to the physiological response.

**Diagnostic mechanisms:**
- **Exocrine pancreatic function:** the stimulated volume and bicarbonate concentration/output of pancreatic fluid are measured. Reduced response can support pancreatic exocrine dysfunction, interpreted using locally established reference ranges and technique.
- **Gastrinoma:** secretin can provoke gastrin release from gastrinoma tissue. The resulting serum gastrin response is assessed against baseline; the label describes an increase of at least 110 pg/mL as the optimal discriminant in the supporting analysis.
- **ERCP:** stimulated pancreatic fluid visibly emerging from the major or minor papilla can help the endoscopist identify the ampulla of Vater or accessory papilla and facilitate cannulation.

Secretin is therefore a diagnostic pharmacological challenge agent, not a direct antitumour drug.

## 5. FDA-approved human secretin product: ChiRhoStim

**Labelled indications**
- Stimulation of pancreatic secretions, including bicarbonate, to aid diagnosis of exocrine pancreas dysfunction.
- Stimulation of gastrin secretion to aid diagnosis of gastrinoma.
- Stimulation of pancreatic secretions to facilitate identification of the ampulla of Vater and accessory papilla during ERCP.

**Labelled doses**
- Exocrine pancreatic function: 0.2 mcg/kg IV over 1 minute.
- Gastrinoma testing: 0.4 mcg/kg IV over 1 minute.
- ERCP papilla identification: 0.2 mcg/kg IV over 1 minute.

**Dosage form and reconstitution**
- Sterile, nonpyrogenic, white lyophilized powder in single-dose vials.
- 16 mcg vial: reconstitute with 8 mL 0.9% sodium chloride to yield 2 mcg/mL.
- 40 mcg vial: reconstitute with 10 mL 0.9% sodium chloride to yield 4 mcg/mL.
- Use immediately after reconstitution and discard unused portion.
- Store at -20°C and protect from light, according to the label.

These details summarize a prescription label for trained clinical teams; they are not instructions for self-administration.

## 6. Porcine secretin products and regulatory history

- Biologically derived porcine secretin was FDA-approved in 1981 and used as a diagnostic agent. FDA review documents record that the prior biologically derived product was discontinued by its manufacturer in 1999.
- Synthetic porcine secretin was developed as a chemically defined alternative and received US approval in 2002 under SecreFlo.
- FDA records document separate indications/approvals associated with pancreatic exocrine function, gastrinoma evaluation, and facilitation of papilla identification during ERCP.
- The historical FDA pharmacology review describes synthetic porcine secretin as a 27-amino-acid peptide identical in sequence to natural porcine secretin.
- A historical SEC filing records a SecreFlo trade-name change request to SecreMax and subsequent reinstatement of SecreFlo; this is brand-history context, not evidence of current availability.

Current US marketing status of SecreFlo, any later discontinuation or withdrawal action, and the status of biologically derived porcine products must be checked directly against the latest Drugs@FDA/Orange Book records before a definitive current-availability statement is made.

## 7. Pharmacokinetics (human synthetic product)

The ChiRhoStim label reports, following a single 0.4 mcg/kg IV bolus in 12 healthy subjects:
- elimination half-life: approximately 45 minutes;
- plasma concentrations declined to baseline within 90–120 minutes;
- clearance: 580.9 ± 51.3 mL/min;
- volume of distribution: 2.7 L.

These values are from the labelled human synthetic product and should not automatically be assigned to porcine secretin without supporting comparative evidence.

## 8. Clinical evidence and interpretation

The human product label describes:
- crossover comparisons of synthetic human, synthetic porcine and biologically derived porcine secretin in patients with chronic pancreatitis;
- a healthy-volunteer cohort in which mean peak bicarbonate concentration was 100 mEq/L after human secretin;
- a gastrinoma analysis using a serum gastrin increase of at least 110 pg/mL over baseline as the optimal discriminator;
- a randomized, placebo-controlled crossover ERCP study in 24 patients with pancreas divisum, in which minor-duct cannulation was successful in 16/24 after secretin versus 2/24 after placebo.

These results are specific to the studies and methods described in the label. Secretin stimulation test results vary with collection method, operator technique, medications, patient population and locally established reference ranges. The label recommends that institutions develop proficiency and normal response ranges rather than importing a universal cutoff without validation.

## 9. Safety, precautions and interactions

The current ChiRhoStim label lists nausea, vomiting, flushing and upset stomach among the most common adverse reactions (reported in at least two patients in the clinical trial dataset). It lists no formal contraindications.

Important test-interpretation precautions:
- Anticholinergic drugs may blunt response and create a false impression of pancreatic dysfunction; the label recommends discontinuation for at least five half-lives before testing when clinically appropriate.
- H2-receptor antagonists and proton-pump inhibitors can increase gastrin response and may falsely suggest gastrinoma. Follow the product label for drug-specific washout guidance.
- Vagotomy and inflammatory bowel disease may be associated with hyporesponse.
- Alcoholic or other liver disease may produce a hyperresponse in pancreatic secretion and mask coexisting pancreatic disease.
- Pregnancy and lactation data for synthetic human secretin are insufficient; consult the current label.

No contraindication in the label does not mean risk-free use. Testing should be performed by appropriately experienced clinicians with interpretation in clinical context.

## 10. Formulation and quality

Human ChiRhoStim is described as a pure, sterile, nonpyrogenic lyophilized powder acetate salt. Labelled excipients include L-cysteine hydrochloride, mannitol and sodium chloride. The 16 mcg and 40 mcg vial presentations have different reconstitution volumes and concentrations.

The historical synthetic porcine FDA review discusses chemical synthesis, biological potency comparison with the prior porcine reference product, and product-quality/manufacturing questions. These review-era findings should not be presented as a current quality assessment of any marketed batch.
