# Semax

> Workbook entry: **115/128** · Curation status: **in_progress**

## Overview

Synthetic ACTH(4–7)-derived heptapeptide used clinically in Russia for selected neurological indications; it is not FDA-approved. Evidence quality and approved indication vary by jurisdiction.

## Identity and chemistry

Sequence: Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro (MEHFPGP), a fragment corresponding to ACTH(4–7) with a C-terminal Pro-Gly-Pro extension. Confirm salt/acetylation form for any specific registered product.

## Mechanism of action

The precise therapeutic molecular target is not established. Preclinical studies report changes in neurotrophin and receptor gene expression, including BDNF, NGF and Trk-family transcripts after experimental cerebral ischemia. These findings are not proof of a direct receptor target or clinical mechanism.

## Pharmacodynamics

Most mechanistic pharmacodynamic data are preclinical. Human reports primarily concern neurological outcomes in regional clinical literature; contemporary independently replicated dose-response studies are lacking.

## Pharmacokinetics

Intranasal products are used in Russian clinical practice. A robust, independently validated human PK profile suitable for extrapolation to unregulated products is not established.

## Clinical evidence

Published Russian studies report use in ischemic stroke and cognitive/neurological conditions, but trial reporting, blinding, randomization and independent replication are limited. No evidence supports nootropic use in healthy people.

## Safety and tolerability

Safety reporting in the clinical literature is incomplete. FDA has identified potential immunogenicity concerns for compounded Semax due to aggregation and peptide-related impurities. Unregulated research supply is not equivalent to a registered medicine.

## Regulatory status

Not FDA-approved and not centrally EMA-authorized. Russian registration/use is reported in literature and secondary regulatory sources; verify current product-specific registration and indication against the Russian state register before treating it as confirmed.

## Formulation and manufacturing

Reported Russian medicinal presentation is intranasal. Product concentration, excipients and quality controls must be taken from the specific authorized product label; research-grade material is not equivalent.

## Bioinformatics and translational research

Potential work: neurotrophin signalling (BDNF/NGF/NT-3), Trk receptor networks, ischemia transcriptomics and peptide stability. Separate preclinical expression changes from clinical efficacy.
