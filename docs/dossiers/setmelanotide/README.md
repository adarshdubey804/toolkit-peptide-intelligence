## 4. Mechanism of action

Setmelanotide is an MC4R agonist. MC4R is a G-protein-coupled receptor expressed in central nervous system circuits involved in energy homeostasis.

The melanocortin pathway conveys satiety and energy-balance signals:
1. Leptin and insulin-related signals influence hypothalamic pro-opiomelanocortin (POMC) neurons.
2. POMC is processed into melanocortin peptides, including α-MSH.
3. α-MSH activates MC4R in downstream neural circuits.
4. MC4R signalling contributes to reduced food intake and increased energy expenditure.

Setmelanotide acts downstream of several upstream pathway defects. It can restore MC4R signalling in certain patients with impaired POMC, PCSK1 or LEPR pathway function, and in Bardet–Biedl syndrome under the approved indication. The exact gene list, diagnostic criteria and age thresholds are label-version-specific.

Do not describe setmelanotide as a general agonist of all melanocortin receptors. Quantitative receptor selectivity and functional potency require primary assay evidence.

## 9. Regulatory history and approved indications

### United States
- FDA approval: 2020.
- Initial indication included chronic weight management in adults and paediatric patients aged 6 years and older with obesity due to POMC, PCSK1 or LEPR deficiency, as specified in the initial label.

## 11. Safety profile

- skin hyperpigmentation and darkening of existing nevi/freckles;
- development of new melanocytic nevi;
- spontaneous penile erections and other sexual arousal effects;
- depression and suicidal ideation/behaviour monitoring;
- hypersensitivity reactions;
- injection-site reactions;
- nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea and abdominal symptoms;
- headache and fatigue.

The current label includes skin examination considerations because of pigmentary changes.
