# Dossier 076 — Teduglutide (Gattex)

**Workbook entry:** 076  
**Category:** Gastrointestinal  
**Workbook indication:** Short bowel syndrome  
**FDA approval:** 2012  
**Curation status:** `in_progress`

## 1. Identity and classification
Teduglutide is a recombinant/synthetic GLP-2 analogue peptide used to improve intestinal absorption in adults and paediatric patients aged 1 year and older with short bowel syndrome (SBS) who are dependent on parenteral support. US brand: Gattex. It is administered subcutaneously. It is structurally related to endogenous glucagon-like peptide-2 (GLP-2).

## 2. Sequence and chemical identity
Teduglutide is a 33-amino-acid analogue of human GLP-2 with an alanine substitution at position 2 (Ala2 replacing Gly2), which increases resistance to DPP-4 degradation and extends biological activity. Sequence of human GLP-2 (1–33): H-Ala-Asp-Gly-Ser-Phe-Ser-Asp-Glu-Met-Asn-Thr-Ile-Leu-Asp-Asn-Leu-Ala-Ala-Arg-Asp-Phe-Ile-Asn-Trp-Leu-Ile-Gln-Thr-Lys-Ile-Thr-Asp-OH. Teduglutide differs at position 2: **H-Ala-Ala-Gly-Ser-Phe-Ser-Asp-Glu-Met-Asn-Thr-Ile-Leu-Asp-Asn-Leu-Ala-Ala-Arg-Asp-Phe-Ile-Asn-Trp-Leu-Ile-Gln-Thr-Lys-Ile-Thr-Asp-OH**.  
Formula and mass should be cross-checked against the current product label and whether the material is reported as free peptide or salt.

## 3. Mechanism of action
Teduglutide binds and activates GLP-2 receptors (GLP2R), a class B GPCR expressed in the gastrointestinal tract and associated cell populations. Signalling is predominantly Gs–adenylyl cyclase–cAMP dependent. GLP-2 receptor activation promotes mucosal growth and increases villus height and crypt depth, may improve intestinal barrier function, reduce gastric motility and secretion, and enhance intestinal fluid/nutrient absorption. The precise contribution of each downstream pathway to clinical benefit is not fully resolved.

## 4. Pharmacodynamics
In SBS, increased absorptive surface and intestinal function can reduce dependence on parenteral nutrition and intravenous fluids. Label studies report increased gastrointestinal fluid absorption and mucosal trophic effects. Changes in parenteral support are clinical outcomes; mucosal histology and absorption measures are supportive pharmacodynamic evidence.

## 5. Pharmacokinetics
After subcutaneous administration, absolute bioavailability in healthy subjects is approximately 88%, with peak concentrations at 3–5 hours. In SBS subjects after 0.05 mg/kg, label-reported median Cmax was approximately 36 ng/mL and steady-state AUCτ approximately 0.15 μg·h/mL. Teduglutide undergoes proteolytic degradation and renal elimination; exposure is increased in renal impairment, requiring label-directed adjustment. ## 6. Clinical evidence
The pivotal STEPS trial (NCT00798967) was a 24-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in adults with SBS dependent on parenteral support. The primary endpoint was a response defined by at least a 20% reduction in weekly parenteral support volume from baseline at weeks 20 and 24. Approximately 63% of teduglutide-treated patients versus 30% of placebo recipients met this response definition. Some participants achieved clinically meaningful reductions in parenteral support; complete independence from parenteral nutrition was uncommon and not guaranteed. Long-term extension studies provide additional duration data but are not equivalent to randomized comparisons.

## 7. Safety and warnings
The US label includes a warning regarding acceleration of neoplastic growth. Before treatment, evaluate for gastrointestinal malignancy and remove polyps as specified in the label; ongoing surveillance is required. Other important risks include intestinal obstruction, biliary and pancreatic disease, fluid overload, and increased absorption of concomitant oral medicines. Common adverse reactions include abdominal pain/distension, nausea, injection-site reactions, fluid overload, and stoma complications. Use the exact current label for monitoring requirements and contraindications.

## 8. Formulation and quality
Gattex is supplied as lyophilized powder for reconstitution for subcutaneous injection. Product-specific controls include peptide identity and sequence, Ala2 substitution, purity, potency, related peptides, degradation products, reconstitution characteristics, sterility, endotoxin, particulate matter, and container closure. Do not equate a research GLP-2 peptide with the approved Gattex drug product.

## 9. Regulatory status
Initial US approval: 2012. The 2024 FDA label indicates adults and paediatric patients aged 1 year and older with SBS who are dependent on parenteral support. Current label includes age-specific evaluation and monitoring. Other jurisdictions may have different product names and age limits.
