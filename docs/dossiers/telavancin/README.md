# Dossier 085 — Telavancin (Vibativ)

**Workbook entry:** 085 | **Category:** Infectious disease | **US approval:** 2009 | **Status:** FDA-approved  
**Curation:** `in_progress`

## Identity and molecular class
Telavancin is a semisynthetic lipoglycopeptide antibacterial derived from the glycopeptide scaffold of vancomycin. Chemical modifications include a lipophilic decylaminoethyl side chain and a phosphonomethyl aminomethyl substituent. It is a complex glycopeptide natural-product derivative, not a simple linear peptide. Do not assign a linear sequence or treat its scaffold as a conventional protein.

## Mechanism
Telavancin inhibits bacterial cell-wall synthesis by binding lipid II/peptidoglycan precursors and interfering with polymerization and cross-linking. Its lipophilic substituent also contributes to disruption of bacterial membrane integrity. These complementary actions support bactericidal activity against susceptible Gram-positive organisms, including MRSA. Resistance mechanisms can overlap with glycopeptide resistance and must be assessed by susceptibility testing.

## Pharmacology
Telavancin is administered intravenously. It is highly protein bound and eliminated predominantly through the kidneys; renal function is important for exposure and dose adjustment. Pharmacodynamic activity is related to AUC/MIC. It is not a treatment for Gram-negative infection. Exact PK parameters and renal adjustment thresholds must be transcribed from the current Vibativ label rather than inferred from vancomycin.

## Clinical evidence and indications
Vibativ is indicated for adults with complicated skin and skin structure infections caused by susceptible Gram-positive bacteria and for hospital-acquired/ventilator-associated bacterial pneumonia caused by susceptible *S. aureus* when alternative treatments are not suitable, according to current US label wording. Trials compared telavancin with vancomycin for complicated skin infections and hospital-acquired pneumonia. The pneumonia evidence and benefit-risk balance require careful attention to renal function and comparator limitations.

## Safety
Important risks include nephrotoxicity and increased mortality in patients with pre-existing moderate/severe renal impairment treated for hospital-acquired/ventilator-associated pneumonia. The label includes a boxed warning related to this population. Other risks include foamy urine/proteinuria, taste disturbance, nausea, vomiting, infusion reactions, QTc effects and hypersensitivity. Pregnancy-related fetal risk is also a critical label warning. Review the current label before summarizing use in pregnancy or renal impairment.

## Formulation and quality
Vibativ is supplied as a sterile lyophilized IV product for reconstitution and infusion. Critical attributes include identity, potency, related substances, glycopeptide impurity profile, residual solvents, water content, reconstitution characteristics, sterility, endotoxin, particulate matter and container closure integrity.

## Regulatory status
FDA approved in 2009. Indication wording and restrictions differ by infection type; hospital-acquired/ventilator-associated pneumonia use is restricted to adults when alternative treatments are not suitable. Do not generalize this to all pneumonia or all Gram-positive infections.
