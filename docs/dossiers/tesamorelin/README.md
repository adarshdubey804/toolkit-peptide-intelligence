# Tesamorelin (Egrifta) — dossier 018

**Curation status:** `in_progress`  
**Entity class:** synthetic, N-terminally modified 44-residue growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH/GRF) analogue.  
**US regulatory status:** FDA-licensed biologic; initial US approval 10 November 2010.  
**Approved-use boundary:** reduction of excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected adults with lipodystrophy. It is explicitly **not** indicated for general weight-loss management.

## 1. Identity and nomenclature

- INN: tesamorelin.
- Product names/presentations: Egrifta (original 1 mg/vial), Egrifta SV (2 mg/vial), Egrifta WR (11.6 mg/vial).
- Sponsor/applicant: Theratechnologies Inc.
- Pharmacological class: growth hormone-releasing factor analogue.
- Target: human GHRH/GRF receptor on pituitary somatotroph cells.
- Route: subcutaneous.
- Initial US approval: 2010; FDA Purple Book BLA 022505.
- The three presentations have different reconstitution instructions and must not share a single product-level dosing record.

## 2. Molecular identity and sequence

Tesamorelin is based on the 44-amino-acid sequence of human growth hormone-releasing factor (GRF), with a trans-3-hexenoyl (C6 unsaturated) moiety attached to the N-terminal tyrosine residue. It is supplied as an acetate salt.

The label structural diagram gives the peptide sequence:

`Y-A-D-A-I-F-T-N-S-Y-R-K-V-L-G-Q-L-S-A-R-K-L-L-Q-D-I-M-S-R-Q-Q-G-E-S-N-Q-E-R-G-A-R-A-R-L-NH2`

The N-terminal tyrosine is modified by the hexenoyl moiety; the C-terminus is amidated. Preserve this covalent modification and C-terminal amide in canonical structure records. Confirm stereochemistry and exact structural graph against the FDA structural formula before registering a canonical SMILES/InChI.

## 4. Mechanism of action

Tesamorelin binds and stimulates human GRF receptors with potency similar to endogenous GRF in vitro.

Mechanistic sequence:
1. Tesamorelin activates GHRH/GRF receptors on pituitary somatotroph cells.
2. Receptor signalling stimulates synthesis and pulsatile secretion of endogenous growth hormone (GH).
3. GH acts on multiple tissues, including adipose tissue, liver, muscle, bone and cartilage.
4. Some GH effects are mediated through increased hepatic and peripheral insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1).

The clinical effect on visceral adipose tissue is downstream of GH-axis stimulation. This is not direct GLP-1 receptor agonism, direct MC4R agonism or administration of exogenous GH.

## 5. Pharmacodynamics

The FDA label reports increased GH secretion and subsequent increases in IGF-1 and IGF-binding protein 3 (IGFBP-3). In clinical trials, no clinically significant changes were observed in TSH, LH, ACTH or prolactin levels.

Curate separately:
- visceral adipose tissue (VAT) response;
- abdominal subcutaneous adipose tissue response;
- total body fat and lean body mass;
- IGF-1 and IGFBP-3 response;
- glucose homeostasis and insulin sensitivity;
- durability after discontinuation.

The label warns that tesamorelin is not a weight-loss medicine; it has a weight-neutral effect in the indicated population.

## 6. Pharmacokinetics

The label describes low absolute bioavailability after subcutaneous injection; the original 1 mg/vial formulation study reported absolute bioavailability below 4% after a 2 mg dose in healthy adults. Do not transfer formulation-specific PK values across Egrifta, Egrifta SV and Egrifta WR without confirming comparability.

## 8. Formulations and presentations

### Egrifta SV
- Single-dose vial containing 2 mg tesamorelin (approximately 2.2 mg tesamorelin acetate).
- Label excipients: histidine, mannitol, polysorbate 20 and sucrose.
- Preservative-free lyophilised powder.
- Reconstitution: 0.5 mL Sterile Water for Injection; label concentration 2 mg/0.5 mL.

### Egrifta WR
- Single-patient-use vial containing 11.6 mg tesamorelin (approximately 11.9 mg tesamorelin acetate).
- Label excipients: hydroxypropyl betadex and mannitol.
- Preservative-free lyophilised powder.
- Reconstitution: 1.3 mL bacteriostatic water for injection; label concentration 8 mg/mL. The supplied diluent contains benzyl alcohol.

### Original Egrifta
The original 1 mg/vial presentation has separate prescribing information and preparation instructions. Do not extrapolate the SV or WR preparation instructions to it.

## 9. Regulatory history and indication

- FDA BLA 022505, initial approval 10 November 2010.
- FDA Purple Book lists Egrifta, Egrifta SV and Egrifta WR presentations under the tesamorelin proper name.
- Current US label indication: reduction of excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected adult patients with lipodystrophy.
- Limitations include lack of established long-term cardiovascular safety and no indication for general weight-loss management.

## 11. Safety profile

Label warnings/precautions include:
- increased risk of neoplasms and monitoring of patients with active malignancy;
- elevated IGF-1 levels;
- fluid retention and symptoms related to GH activity;
- glucose intolerance or diabetes mellitus;
- hypersensitivity reactions;
- injection-site reactions;
- increased mortality in patients with acute critical illness (GH-class warning context).

Extract the full contraindication list, exact warning language, adverse-event frequencies, discontinuation rates and monitoring recommendations from the current presentation-specific label. Include immunogenicity and anti-drug antibody data.

## 12. Drug interactions

The label includes interaction considerations for:
- cytochrome P450-metabolised drugs, because changes in IGF-1/GH may affect CYP450 activity;
- glucocorticoids, which can influence the GH/IGF-1 axis and treatment response.

Capture the exact label evidence, affected drugs and clinical recommendations. Do not add unsupported interaction claims.
