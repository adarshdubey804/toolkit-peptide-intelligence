# Dossier 027 — Triptorelin (triptorelin acetate / triptorelin pamoate)

**Curation status:** in progress — FDA substance identity and US product labels plus UK SmPCs reviewed; full indication-level clinical evidence, global authorisation map, immunogenicity and CMC still require extraction.  
**Asset class:** synthetic decapeptide analogue of gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH/LHRH).  
**Pharmacology:** GnRH receptor agonist; continuous exposure suppresses pituitary gonadotropins and gonadal sex steroids after an initial stimulatory phase.  
**US product examples:** TRELSTAR (monthly/3-month/6-month pamoate presentations) and TRIPTODUR (24-week pamoate depot for central precocious puberty).  
**UK/EU product example:** Decapeptyl SR (triptorelin pamoate/embonate; multiple depot presentations).

## 1. Identity and classification

Triptorelin is a synthetic decapeptide analogue of native GnRH. It is supplied in different salt and release forms, notably soluble triptorelin acetate and poorly soluble triptorelin pamoate (also called embonate). The salt and dosage form must be recorded separately from the active peptide moiety.

## 2. Mechanism of action

Triptorelin is an agonist at pituitary GnRH receptors. Its response is biphasic and depends on sustained exposure:

1. **Initial stimulation:** activation of GnRH receptors causes a transient increase in LH and FSH. Testosterone in males and oestradiol in females may temporarily rise.
2. **Continuous exposure:** prolonged agonist exposure causes pituitary GnRH receptor desensitisation/downregulation and reduced LH/FSH secretion.
3. **Downstream suppression:** reduced gonadotropins suppress testicular or ovarian steroidogenesis. This is used in hormone-dependent prostate cancer and in central precocious puberty, among other product-specific indications.

The current TRIPTODUR label states that after the first administration there is a transient surge in LH, FSH, testosterone and estradiol; after chronic continuous administration, sustained decreases in LH/FSH and marked reductions in sex steroids are observed by about four weeks. [3]

## 3. Molecular structure and structure–activity features

Triptorelin is a decapeptide with sequence:

`pGlu-His-Trp-Ser-Tyr-D-Trp-Leu-Arg-Pro-Gly-NH₂`

The key substitution relative to native mammalian GnRH is **D-tryptophan at position 6** (native GnRH has glycine at position 6). The C-terminal glycinamide and N-terminal pyroglutamate are retained. This substitution contributes to the analogue's potent GnRH agonist activity and altered stability; quantitative SAR attribution requires primary comparative data.

The FDA TRIPTODUR label gives the chemical name as 5-oxo-L-prolyl-L-histidyl-L-tryptophyl-L-seryl-L-tyrosyl-D-tryptophyl-L-leucyl-L-arginyl-L-prolylglycine amide (pamoate salt). [3]

**Salt distinction:** triptorelin pamoate/embonate is a salt of the peptide with pamoic acid. A pamoate product's total formula and molecular weight include the counterion and must not be substituted for the free peptide's formula/mass. The FDA label reports 1,699.9 Da for the pamoate salt in TRIPTODUR. [3]

## 4. Receptor pharmacology and pharmacodynamics

The primary target is the pituitary GnRH receptor, a class A GPCR. Receptor activation engages Gq/11-associated phospholipase-C signalling, intracellular calcium mobilisation and downstream pathways controlling gonadotropin synthesis and secretion.

With continuous therapeutic exposure, pituitary responsiveness falls, LH/FSH release is suppressed and gonadal steroid concentrations decrease. The clinical endpoint and degree of suppression depend on indication, depot formulation, dose interval and patient factors.

A complete quantitative receptor profile (binding affinity, functional EC50, intrinsic efficacy, species comparison and signalling bias) has not been verified in this curation pass and remains unresolved.

## 5. Pharmacokinetics and depot formulations

Triptorelin has multiple formulations; PK values must not be transferred between them.

### Immediate-release / soluble forms

Soluble triptorelin acetate has been used in short-acting protocols. A UK SmPC reports after a 100 μg subcutaneous dose in healthy volunteers a Tmax of approximately 0.63 hours and an elimination half-life of about 7.6 hours; these data describe the short-acting formulation, not depot release. [4]

### Sustained-release forms

- **TRELSTAR:** US intramuscular pamoate depot presentations include 3.75 mg (approximately monthly), 11.25 mg (approximately every 12 weeks) and 22.5 mg (approximately every 24 weeks), with product-specific instructions and indications. [5]
- **TRIPTODUR:** 22.5 mg triptorelin (equivalent to 31 mg triptorelin pamoate) in a biodegradable microsphere formulation, administered intramuscularly every 24 weeks for central precocious puberty. The label reports a burst phase followed by maintenance release; after two injections 24 weeks apart, no apparent accumulation was observed in the studied children. [3,6]
- **Decapeptyl SR 11.25 mg:** UK product information describes a 3-month depot; following injection, plasma concentrations peak during the first hours, then decline and remain measurable through the 3-month interval. [7]
- **Decapeptyl SR 22.5 mg:** UK product information describes a 6-month depot. In prostate cancer patients, median Tmax was 3 hours (range 2–12) and Cmax 40.0 ng/mL (reported range 22.2–76.8); the SmPC reports no accumulation over 12 months of treatment. [8]

The elimination half-life after intravenous administration (about 2.8 hours in one healthy-volunteer dataset) is not the same as the apparent duration of a depot product. [8]

## 6. Clinical uses and product-specific indications

### United States

- **TRELSTAR:** palliative treatment of advanced prostate cancer. Product strengths and injection intervals are formulation-specific. [5]
- **TRIPTODUR:** treatment of paediatric patients with central precocious puberty (CPP). The current US label specifies 22.5 mg intramuscularly once every 24 weeks and requires clinical/laboratory monitoring of pubertal suppression. [3,6]

### United Kingdom

The Decapeptyl SR SmPCs list product-specific indications. The 11.25 mg product includes locally advanced non-metastatic prostate cancer as an alternative to surgical castration; other Decapeptyl formulations have their own indication lists. Do not assume that a UK indication is authorised for every strength or in the United States. [7,8]

### Other uses

Triptorelin has been used in reproductive medicine and in selected gynaecological or endocrine indications in some jurisdictions. Such uses must be tied to a named product, national label and clinical protocol; they should not be represented as universal indications.

## 7. Regulatory status

**United States:** FDA-approved TRELSTAR and TRIPTODUR products. FDA review records state that a 1-month triptorelin pamoate formulation was initially approved in 2000 for palliative treatment of advanced prostate cancer; TRIPTODUR is also an FDA-approved 24-week product for CPP. [3,5,9]

**United Kingdom:** Decapeptyl SR products have current UK SmPCs, including 11.25 mg and 22.5 mg presentations. Authorised indications differ by presentation. [7,8]

**Other jurisdictions:** not exhaustively mapped in this dossier. EMA/national, MHRA, PMDA, CDSCO and other regulator records should be verified separately. FDA GSRS identity records are not approval records. [1,2]

## 8. Safety and tolerability

Safety is associated with the initial hormonal flare and subsequent sex-steroid suppression. Product labels and SmPCs identify clinically important domains including:
- initial tumour flare and possible worsening of prostate-cancer symptoms, including urinary obstruction or spinal cord compression risk in susceptible patients;
- hot flushes, sweating, decreased libido and sexual dysfunction;
- bone mineral density reduction with prolonged hypogonadism;
- metabolic changes and hyperglycaemia/diabetes risk in men receiving GnRH agonists;
- cardiovascular events and QT/QTc considerations;
- mood changes/depression and rare neurological events described in GnRH-agonist labeling;
- injection-site reactions, sterile abscess or depot/device complications;
- hypersensitivity reactions;
- paediatric monitoring for pubertal suppression, growth and bone age in CPP.

The above is a safety-domain summary, not a complete frequency table. Incidence, severity and contraindications must be extracted from the current label for each formulation. [3,5,7,8]

## 9. Formulation, administration and quality

TRIPTODUR is a lyophilised biodegradable microgranule formulation for intramuscular suspension. Each vial contains 22.5 mg triptorelin (equivalent to 31 mg triptorelin pamoate), with carboxymethylcellulose sodium, mannitol, poly-D,L-lactide-co-glycolide (PLGA) and polysorbate 80; it is reconstituted with the supplied 2 mL sterile water and administered as a single IM injection. [3]

Other depot products use distinct strengths, excipients, polymer matrices and release profiles. The 3-, 6- and 12-month product labels must be treated independently.
