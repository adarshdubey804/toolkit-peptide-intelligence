## Identity and chemistry
Trofinetide is a synthetic analogue of the N-terminal tripeptide of insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1), commonly described as a glycine-proline-glutamate (GPE) analogue with a modified terminal group. It is an orally administered small peptide-like molecule developed for Rett syndrome. It is not full-length IGF-1 and should not be represented as an IGF-1 receptor agonist without evidence. ## Mechanism
The precise mechanism by which trofinetide produces clinical benefit in Rett syndrome is not fully understood. The molecule was developed from IGF-1-related neurotrophic biology, and preclinical studies have explored synaptic function, neuroinflammation, and neuronal support. These proposed mechanisms must remain hypotheses unless directly established in humans. Do not claim that trofinetide replaces IGF-1, normalizes MECP2 function, or acts through a proven single receptor.

## Pharmacology and PK
Trofinetide is administered orally as a solution. The current label should be used for exposure, food effects, renal elimination, and dose modification in renal impairment. ## Clinical evidence
Daybue is indicated for treatment of Rett syndrome in adults and paediatric patients aged 2 years and older. The pivotal LAVENDER trial (NCT04181723) was a 12-week randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in females with Rett syndrome. Co-primary endpoints were change from baseline in the Rett Syndrome Behaviour Questionnaire (RSBQ) and Clinical Global Impression–Improvement (CGI-I). Trofinetide showed statistically significant improvement versus placebo on both measures. These scales capture symptom severity/global change; the trial does not establish cure, reversal of the underlying genetic cause, or disease modification over the long term.

## Safety
Common adverse reactions include diarrhoea and vomiting; weight loss, decreased appetite, fever and seizures are also important label safety considerations. Diarrhoea can be severe and may require interruption or discontinuation under the label. Monitor hydration and nutritional status, particularly in patients with low baseline weight or feeding difficulties. The label includes warnings regarding hypersensitivity and interactions/clinical effects with medicines affecting gastrointestinal motility or hydration; use current prescribing information for exact wording.

## Formulation and quality
Daybue is supplied as an oral solution. Product controls include identity, stereochemical purity, assay, related substances, degradation products, solution pH, concentration uniformity, microbial limits, preservative system if present, and container integrity. Do not assume compounded or research-grade material is equivalent to the approved product.
