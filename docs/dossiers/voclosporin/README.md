# Dossier 098 — Voclosporin (Lupkynis)

**Workbook entry:** 098 | **Category:** Immunology, inflammation & transplant | **First US approval:** 2021 | **Status:** FDA-approved  
**Curation:** `in_progress`

## Identity and chemistry
Voclosporin is a semisynthetic cyclosporine analogue and cyclic undecapeptide calcineurin inhibitor. It contains a modified amino-acid side chain relative to cyclosporine A, designed to alter potency and pharmacokinetics. It is a cyclic, highly lipophilic peptide with multiple N-methylated/non-proteinogenic residues; do not model it as a linear peptide. Lupkynis is an oral formulation.

## Mechanism
Voclosporin binds cyclophilin to form a complex that inhibits calcineurin phosphatase. This reduces NFAT dephosphorylation and nuclear translocation, suppressing IL-2 and other cytokine transcription and T-cell activation. In lupus nephritis, the immunosuppressive effect is combined with a direct podocyte-stabilizing hypothesis; do not present the podocyte mechanism as the sole or definitively proven clinical mechanism.

## Pharmacology and PK
Voclosporin is orally administered and metabolized primarily by CYP3A4. It is a substrate of CYP3A4 and P-glycoprotein; strong CYP3A4 inhibitors are contraindicated and moderate inhibitors require label-directed adjustment. Exposure can be altered by strong inducers. It has a relatively predictable exposure profile compared with older cyclosporine formulations, but drug interactions and renal function remain important. Use the current label for exact half-life and exposure data.

## Clinical evidence and indication
Lupkynis is indicated in combination with a background immunosuppressive regimen for adults with active lupus nephritis. The pivotal AURORA 1 trial demonstrated a higher complete renal response rate at week 52 with voclosporin plus mycophenolate mofetil and low-dose glucocorticoids than placebo plus background therapy. AURORA 2 extension evaluated longer-term outcomes. Do not claim a cure for SLE or extrapolate the indication to all nephritis classes without label context.

## Safety
Important risks include serious infection, malignancy, nephrotoxicity, hypertension, hyperkalaemia and neurotoxicity, consistent with calcineurin inhibition. The label includes a boxed warning concerning serious infections and malignancy associated with immunosuppression. Avoid use in severe renal impairment and uncontrolled hypertension according to label restrictions. Monitor renal function, blood pressure, potassium and interacting medicines.

## Formulation and quality
Lupkynis is supplied as oral soft-gelatin capsules. Critical attributes include cyclic peptide identity, stereochemical integrity, impurity profile, assay, dissolution, content uniformity, stability and capsule integrity.

## Regulatory status
FDA approved January 2021 for adults with active lupus nephritis in combination with background immunosuppression. Verify current label and patient-selection criteria.
