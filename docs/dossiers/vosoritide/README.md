# Dossier 107 — Vosoritide (Voxzogo)

**Workbook entry:** 107 | **Category:** Rare & genetic disease | **First US approval:** 2021 | **Status:** FDA-approved  
**Curation:** `in_progress`

## Identity and sequence architecture
Vosoritide is a recombinant 39-amino-acid analogue of human C-type natriuretic peptide (CNP). It consists of the 37 C-terminal amino acids of human CNP53 with an additional Pro-Gly dipeptide at the N-terminus to confer resistance to neutral endopeptidase (NEP) degradation. It is manufactured in *Escherichia coli* using recombinant DNA technology. The FDA label reports formula C176H290N56O51S3 and molecular weight approximately 4.1 kDa. Preserve disulfide connectivity and stereochemistry from the official structure diagram when creating machine-readable structure records.

## Mechanism
Vosoritide binds natriuretic peptide receptor-B (NPR-B/guanylyl cyclase-B), increasing intracellular cGMP. In achondroplasia, gain-of-function FGFR3 signalling suppresses endochondral bone growth. NPR-B/cGMP signalling antagonizes FGFR3 downstream signalling, including inhibition of RAF-1/MEK/ERK pathway activity, promoting chondrocyte proliferation and differentiation and increasing growth-plate activity. It addresses the growth-plate signalling imbalance; it does not correct the underlying FGFR3 mutation.

## Pharmacology and PK
Vosoritide is administered subcutaneously once daily. The Pro-Gly N-terminal extension reduces NEP-mediated degradation relative to endogenous CNP. It has a short systemic half-life despite this modification; exposure and pharmacodynamic effect are linked to transient NPR-B activation. The label requires attention to hydration and blood pressure because transient decreases in blood pressure can occur. Renal impairment recommendations and age/weight restrictions should be taken from the current label.

## Clinical evidence and indication
Voxzogo is indicated to increase linear growth in paediatric patients with achondroplasia who have open epiphyses. The initial US approval in 2021 covered children aged 5 years and older; a 2023 expansion included younger children, and current label wording should be used for the full paediatric population. The pivotal phase 3 study demonstrated increased annualized growth velocity compared with placebo over 52 weeks. The US indication is under accelerated approval based on improvement in annualized growth velocity; confirmatory clinical benefit requirements should be tracked. Do not describe it as reversing achondroplasia or normalizing adult height.

## Safety
Important adverse reactions include injection-site reactions, vomiting, decreased blood pressure and related symptoms such as dizziness. Ensure label-directed hydration and monitor for symptomatic hypotension. Treatment requires open growth plates; it is not indicated after epiphyseal closure. Long-term skeletal, functional and patient-centred outcomes should be distinguished from short-term growth velocity.

## Formulation and quality
Voxzogo is supplied as sterile, preservative-free lyophilized powder for subcutaneous injection in single-dose vials, with strengths dependent on product presentation. Critical quality attributes include sequence identity, recombinant host/process impurities, disulfide mapping, potency at NPR-B, purity, aggregates, residual host-cell proteins/DNA, sterility, endotoxin, particulate matter and container closure.

## Regulatory status
FDA initial approval: 19 November 2021. Accelerated approval for increasing linear growth in paediatric achondroplasia with open epiphyses. Label and age range have expanded; check current label for the exact current population and renal limitations.
