# Dossier 102 — Zilucoplan (Zilbrysq)

**Workbook entry:** 102 | **Category:** Immunology, inflammation & transplant | **First US approval:** 2023 | **Status:** FDA-approved  
**Curation:** `in_progress`

## Identity and chemistry
Zilucoplan is a synthetic macrocyclic peptide complement inhibitor with a lipidated/albumin-binding design that supports subcutaneous administration and prolonged exposure. It binds complement component C5. The molecule contains non-proteinogenic residues and a defined macrocyclic structure; do not represent it as a linear canonical peptide. Capture exact sequence, stereochemistry, lipid/side-chain architecture, formula and mass from FDA chemistry sources before structure registration.

## Mechanism
Zilucoplan binds C5 and inhibits its cleavage into C5a and C5b. This prevents formation of the terminal complement complex (C5b-9, membrane attack complex) and reduces complement-mediated injury at the neuromuscular junction in acetylcholine-receptor-antibody-positive generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG). It does not directly target the acetylcholine receptor or suppress antibody production.

## Pharmacology
Zilucoplan is administered once daily by subcutaneous injection. It has a prolonged exposure profile supported by its molecular design. Complement inhibition is the pharmacodynamic marker; monitor clinical response and infection risk. Exact PK and immunogenicity values should be extracted from the current label. It is not interchangeable with intravenous monoclonal C5 inhibitors.

## Clinical evidence and indication
Zilbrysq is indicated for adults with anti-AChR antibody-positive generalized myasthenia gravis. The pivotal phase 3 RAISE trial (NCT04115293) demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in MG-ADL score at week 12 versus placebo, with supportive QMG and other outcomes. The indication is restricted to the specified antibody-positive adult gMG population; do not generalize to seronegative disease, ocular-only MG or other complement-mediated disorders.

## Safety
C5 inhibition increases susceptibility to serious infections caused by encapsulated bacteria, including meningococcal infection. The product has a boxed warning for serious meningococcal infections. Vaccination against meningococcal serogroups and other encapsulated pathogens must follow current label requirements; vaccination does not eliminate risk. Other adverse reactions include injection-site reactions, diarrhea and respiratory infections. Review current label for hypersensitivity and immunization requirements.

## Formulation and quality
Zilbrysq is a sterile solution for subcutaneous injection in a prefilled syringe. Critical attributes include macrocycle identity, stereochemistry, side-chain integrity, peptide purity, related substances, potency for C5 inhibition, concentration, pH, sterility, endotoxin, particulates and syringe/container integrity.

## Regulatory status
FDA first approved in 2023 for adult anti-AChR antibody-positive gMG. Maintain the antibody-positive population restriction and boxed warning.
