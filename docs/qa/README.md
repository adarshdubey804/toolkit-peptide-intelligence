## Common completion gates

A dossier should not be marked complete until applicable requirements are met:

1. Canonical identity and chemical structure are validated from authoritative sources, including noncanonical residues, stereochemistry, termini, salts, disulfides, linkers and conjugates.
2. Each scientific claim has a source-specific citation/locator and evidence classification.
3. Current product labels and regulator records are checked by product, indication, jurisdiction and as-of date.
4. PK/PD, clinical endpoints, safety, interactions and special populations are extracted with study context and denominators.
5. Images and structure assets have source attribution, alt text and verified reuse rights.
6. Product/formulation/route distinctions and unresolved conflicts are preserved in the data model.
7. Independent scientific review and production import validation are recorded.
